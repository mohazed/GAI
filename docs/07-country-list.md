## Phase 3: all 193 scored entities

Everyone else gets the **light protocol**: generated indicators (votes, funding, GNI, trade where reported), recognition status (B8) from the recognition table, ICJ/ICC positions from the case lists, plus a 20-minute scan of the foreign ministry site and one news search per indicator group. Assessments record `none-found` with the queries used. Expect 0–5 hand-authored events per country.

## Belligerents and special notes

- **ISR, PSE:** excluded (D-10).
- **IRN, LBN, YEM, SYR:** scored like everyone (the scale measures accountability conduct, not warfare), each with a page note that direct hostilities are outside the scale. Flagged as an open question in `01-plan.md` for the author.
- **VAT (Holy See):** included as observer; its statements are B9 events; no trade or arms indicators (not-applicable is not automatic; record `none-found`).
