**Title:** Proposed: methodology v1.0.0-rc.2, comment until 2026-10-19

The methodology of the Gaza Accountability Index is versioned, and every change is proposed in
public before it is merged (methodology page, Versioning and changelog). This discussion opens
the 14-day comment window for version 1.0.0-rc.2. It closes on **19 October 2026**. The proposal
is merged only after that date, and comments received here are answered in this thread.

Pull request: {PR} (branch `methodology/v1.0.0-rc.2`).

**What it is.** Version 1.0.0 is still a release candidate: the scores are computed and not yet
displayed on the site. Before they are, the author hand-scored ten countries (the United States,
Germany, the United Kingdom, France, Spain, Ireland, South Africa, Türkiye, Egypt and India) and
reviewed every threshold against the 193 scored entities. The worksheets and the measurements
are in `docs/calibration/` of the repository. 1.0.0-rc.2 records the adjustments that review
recommended. Each change applies to every country in the same way.

**The eight rule changes**

1. An event supported by an official video or a parliamentary record can be `confirmed`
   (weight 1.0), as an official release can. Both are the government's own record.
2. Six indicators that are states rather than one-off acts (F-35 supply chain, arms export
   suspension, two-way embargo, ICJ intervention, sanctions on ICC officials, UNRWA funding
   suspension) count once when two records of the same state overlap: the most severe holds.
3. UN General Assembly votes (B1) are capped at −15 and +15 in total, the size of the passivity
   penalty. A state that votes yes and does nothing else stays in the Passive band, as the project
   specification describes it, and no state loses more than the penalty to votes alone.
4. Only an act that adds +2 or more ends the passivity penalty. Under 1.0.0-rc.1 a negative act,
   such as a new agreement with Israel, also ended it, so adding a negative act could raise a
   score.
5. A recognition of the State of Palestine that predates 7 October 2023 (+3) no longer ends the
   passivity penalty: it is a state that predates the period, not engagement in it.
6. Customs and trade values for calendar year 2022 are not used in the period from 7 October
   2023: no computed value rests on data from before the war.
7. Humanitarian funding (D1) counts only contributions that the OCHA Financial Tracking Service
   dates on or after 7 October 2023.
8. Statements (B9, B10) count when made by the head of state, the head of government or the
   foreign minister, the three officials who represent a state without further authorisation
   under the Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties.

The version also states in its text the readings the build already applied (for example how
"most severe" is chosen, and that the name of D1 is a share of GNI), so the site no longer needs
separate notes for them. The changelog entry lists each change with its rationale and its
measured effect.

**Effect.** On 5 October 2026, over the 193 scored entities:

- With the events published so far (the generated ones: votes, vetoes, recognitions, funding,
  trade and arms data), 120 display scores move by 1 point or more; the file `diff.json` of the
  version lists each with the cause. Most are states whose record is votes alone, which move from
  Acting to Passive under change 3.
- With the hand-researched events awaiting review counted as if published, the numbers are
  provisional: 109 display scores move and 64 bands change. Among the ten: Germany −1 → −7, the
  United Kingdom +54 → +52, France +48 → +45, Türkiye +77 → +80, Egypt +23 → +20.

`diff.json` is computed again on the published data before the scores are displayed.

**How to comment.** Reply in this thread, or open an issue in the repository with the label
`methodology`. Please name the indicator or the rule, and, where you can, a document. Comments
on points, thresholds and caps are as welcome as comments on wording. Changes 3, 4 and 8 are
also among the points put to the external reviewers of the methodology once they are named
(docs/08 §2; none is named yet).

**What happens next.** After 19 October 2026 the author decides, with the comments, whether to
merge the proposal as it stands, change it, or withdraw part of it; any change to the proposal
reopens the window for that part. The decision and its reasons are recorded in the changelog.
