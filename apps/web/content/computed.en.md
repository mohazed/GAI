## How the computed indicators are built

The formulas of A1, A2, A4, C3 and D1 leave some choices to the build: which dates a window covers, which records count, which year of a denominator applies. These choices were made when the data fetchers were written, on 27 September 2026, and each is stated here with its reason. The code is in `packages/pipeline/src/fetch` and `packages/pipeline/src/generate` of the public repository.

- **Every dataset response is archived before it is used.** Each response of the OCHA Financial Tracking Service (FTS), the World Bank and UN Comtrade is saved to the Wayback Machine, and the tables in `data/structured/` are built from the archived bytes, never from a separate live request. Reason: every row can then be checked against a public copy, with its SHA-256 hash. For UN Comtrade the data of record are the keyless preview queries (`/public/v1/preview/…`), archived and parsed; queries made with a subscription key are used only to cross-check them. Reason: a query with a key cannot be archived without publishing the key.
- **D1 windows are the twelve calendar months before the month the value applies to.** A D1 value holds for one calendar month; for October 2024 the window is October 2023 to September 2024. Reason: the methodology recomputes D1 monthly over the trailing 12 months, and a window of whole months ending before the month gives each month one value, fixed before the month starts.
- **FTS flows count by their FTS date.** A flow enters every window that contains the date FTS gives it, including the flows dated before 7 October 2023 that FTS lists under the flash appeals for the occupied Palestinian territory. Reason: FTS dates each contribution and lists these flows as funding of the appeals the rule names; the date recorded by FTS is the only date applied, for every donor alike. Windows that include such flows end in September 2024 at the latest.
- **Government flows are attributed by their FTS source location.** A flow counts for a country when its donor organisation has the FTS type "Governments" (the spelling FTS uses; the methodology says "Government") and its source location is that country. Reason: governments fund through several organisation entries, and the source location is the field FTS fills for all of them. A government flow whose source location is missing or is not a scored country is not attributed by name. At the fetch of 27 September 2026 these included flows of the United Kingdom, the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation, the Qatar Fund for Development and the German Federal Foreign Office; each is to be attributed by its FTS organisation id, with the archived FTS organisation record as the reason, and the overrides are listed here once applied (none is applied at this build). Flows of the Palestinian Authority and of Jersey stay unattributed, because neither is a scored country.
- **GNI is the latest World Bank year not after the window, else the most recent year.** Reason: the ratio x = F ÷ GNI then compares funding and income of the same period where the World Bank has published it; where it has not, the most recent year is the only figure available. For some countries the most recent year published is old: Liechtenstein (2009), Eritrea (2011), South Sudan (2015), Yemen (2018), Cuba (2019) and the Syrian Arab Republic (2022). Monaco has no GNI in the World Bank data, so its D1 is no-data for every month in which FTS records funding from it.
- **A4 leaves out the orders of 2023.** Only orders of 2024 or later count. Reason: SIPRI dates orders by year only, so an order of 2023 cannot be placed before or after 7 October 2023; counting none of them avoids counting orders signed before the war.
- **A2 and C3 use calendar-year Comtrade data.** Each value covers one calendar year of trade and holds from the reporter's first release of that year's data (Israel's, for mirror data) to its first release of the next year's. Reason: the annual series is the one UN Comtrade publishes for every reporter, and the release date, read from Comtrade's archived data-availability record, is the day the figures became public.
- **A2 counts HS 8526 and 8802 only where a confirmation is recorded.** The rows of both headings are kept in `comtrade_a2.csv`, and they count for a country only when a licence register, a parliamentary answer or a published investigation citing the customs code, recorded in a table of confirmations, shows that the flow is military. Reason: both headings are mostly civil goods (radar and remote-control apparatus, aircraft). No confirmation is recorded at this build, so headings 8526 and 8802 count for no country.
