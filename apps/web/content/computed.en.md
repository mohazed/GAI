## How the computed indicators are built

The formulas of A1, A2, A4, C3 and D1 leave some choices to the build: which dates a window covers, which records count, which year of a denominator applies. These choices were made when the data fetchers were written, on 27 September 2026, and each is stated here with its reason. The code is in `packages/pipeline/src/fetch` and `packages/pipeline/src/generate` of the public repository. Since version 1.0.0-rc.2 the methodology above states the rules these choices led to (calendar-year trade data and the 2022 values left out, the orders of 2023 left out, the GNI year, FTS flows from 7 October 2023); what remains here is how the build applies them.

- **Every dataset response is archived before it is used.** Each response of the OCHA Financial Tracking Service (FTS), the World Bank and UN Comtrade is saved to the Wayback Machine, and the tables in `data/structured/` are built from the archived bytes, never from a separate live request. Reason: every row can then be checked against a public copy, with its SHA-256 hash. For UN Comtrade the data of record are the keyless preview queries (`/public/v1/preview/…`), archived and parsed; queries made with a subscription key are used only to cross-check them. Reason: a query with a key cannot be archived without publishing the key.
- **D1 windows are the twelve calendar months before the month the value applies to.** A D1 value holds for one calendar month; for October 2024 the window is October 2023 to September 2024. Reason: the methodology recomputes D1 monthly over the trailing 12 months, and a window of whole months ending before the month gives each month one value, fixed before the month starts.
- **Government flows are attributed by their FTS source location.** A flow counts for a country when its donor organisation has the FTS type "Governments" and its source location is that country. Reason: governments fund through several organisation entries, and the source location is the field FTS fills for all of them. A government flow whose source location is missing or is not a scored country is attributed only by its FTS organisation id, when the FTS organisation record (the archived list of FTS organisations) places the organisation in one scored country; otherwise it is not attributed. At this build four organisations are attributed that way: the United Kingdom, Government of (FTS organisation 2917, no source location), 2 flows, USD 13,068,388, to the United Kingdom; the Swiss Development Cooperation/Swiss Humanitarian Aid (2646, no source location), 1 flow, USD 1,670,550, to Switzerland; the Qatar Fund for Development (13052, no source location), 1 flow, USD 318,964, to Qatar; and the German Federal Foreign Office (13808, recorded at the location Occupied Palestinian Territory), 1 flow, USD 91,654, to Germany. Still unattributed at the fetch of 28 September 2026: the Palestinian Authority (1 flow, USD 10,000,000) and Jersey Overseas Aid (21 flows, USD 2,912,802), because neither is a scored country, and the Education Above All Foundation (FTS type "Governments", location "Global", 1 flow, USD 1,000,000), which no organisation record places in one country.
- **Old GNI years.** D1 divides by the GNI of the latest World Bank year not after the window, else of the most recent year. For some countries the most recent year published is old: Liechtenstein (2009), Eritrea (2011), South Sudan (2015), Yemen (2018), Cuba (2019) and the Syrian Arab Republic (2022). Monaco has no GNI in the World Bank data, so its D1 is no-data for every month in which FTS records funding from it.
- **An order SIPRI lists at 0 TIV leaves A4 without data.** When the orders a country placed with Israel in the release's data year add up to 0 TIV, A4 is no-data for that country, not none-found. Reason: SIPRI writes 0 for an order below 0.5 TIV and for an order whose number is not yet known, so whether the order exceeds the formula's "TIV > 0" cannot be read from the release. Such an order adds no points.
- **The release date of an A2 or C3 value is read from UN Comtrade's archived data-availability record.** Reason: it is the day the reporter's figures for the year became public, from which the value holds.
- **A2 counts HS 8526 and 8802 only where a confirmation is recorded.** The rows of both headings are kept in `comtrade_a2.csv`, and they count for a country only when a licence register, a parliamentary answer or a published investigation citing the customs code, recorded in `a2_confirmed_military.csv` (one row per country and heading, citing the archived document), shows that the flow is military. Reason: both headings are mostly civil goods (radar and remote-control apparatus, aircraft). The table holds no row at this build, so headings 8526 and 8802 count for no country.
