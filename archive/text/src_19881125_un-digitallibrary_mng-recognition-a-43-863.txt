ANNES

Statement jasued at Vian Bator on 22 November 1988 by the Ministry
of Foreign Affairs of the Mongolian People's Republic

1. The Mongolian public has warmly welcomed the news that the nineteenth Special
Session of the Palestine National Council decided to declare an independent
Palestinian State. In saluting this historic decisicn, the Mongolian People's
Republic officially declares that it recognizes the declaration of the independent
Palestinian State. The proclamation of the Palestinian State is an event which
will give a significant impetus to bringing years-old heroic struggle of the
Palestinian people to final victory and to a comprehensive and just settlement of
the Middle East crisis.

2. The Government of the Mongolian People's Republic believes that the convening
under the auspices of the United Nations of an international conference on Middle
East problems, and the holding of equal and constructive negotiations among all
parties concerned and, >".ove all, the withdrawal of Israeli troops from the
occupied Arab territories, are of cvucial importaace for the practical
establishment of a recently declared Palestinian State. The Mongolian people
reaffirms its firm solidarity with the just struggle of the Palestinian people for
the full exercise of their legitimate right to establish a State of their own,
