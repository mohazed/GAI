North Macedonia
Tuesday, August 25, 2026
📁 Evidence
North Macedonia has signed an economic consultations memorandum and two bilateral documents with Israel, fostering cooperation in education, science, and sports. This marks a new step in diplomatic relations for the country. Domestically, the 'Lottery' case against former First Deputy Prime Minister Artan Grubi saw significant developments, with the prosecution presenting 231 pieces of evidence and seven witnesses. This legal proceeding is drawing political commentary, including criticism directed at Bujar Osmani. Meanwhile, an incident on Lake Ohrid involving a boat and paddleboarders has led to increased controls, and a new fire in Bojane is causing smoke over Skopje. Further political and legal news includes the 'Talir 2' case concerning VMRO's 'White Palace' nearing its statute of limitations, and opposition leader Venko Filipce's assertion that Prime Minister Hristijan Mickoski will seek elections once the national budget is depleted.
