Message of Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif, Prime Minister of the
Islamic Republic of Pakistan, on the Occasion of the
International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People

(23 November 2026)

On the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People,
Pakistan reaffirms its unwavering commitment to the just cause of
Palestine and to the inalienable rights of its people. This solemn
occasion reminds us not only of the decades of occupation,
dispossession and injustice endured by the Palestinian people, but
also of our collective responsibility to translate international
solidarity into meaningful action.

The Palestinian people have endured immeasurable suffering,
particularly during the devastating conflict in Gaza. More than
74,000 Palestinians have been killed and over 175,000 injured, while
entire communities have been displaced and much of Gaza's civilian
infrastructure reduced to rubble. Yet, amidst this unprecedented
devastation, the Palestinian people have demonstrated extraordinary
courage, resilience and steadfastness in their pursuit of freedom,
dignity and justice.

Diplomatic developments over the past year offered renewed hope
for ending the suffering of the Palestinian people. The New York
Declaration, the Gaza Peace Plan, the adoption of United Nations
Security Council resolution 2803 (2025), and the establishment of the
Board of Peace have provided important frameworks for
consolidating the ceasefire, addressing the humanitarian crisis, and
advancing efforts towards a just and lasting political settlement.
Pakistan has constructively contributed to these efforts, including as
part of the Group of Eight Arab and Islamic countries. Our decision
to join the Board of Peace reflects our commitment to ending the
bloodshed, securing a permanent ceasefire, ensuring unhindered
humanitarian assistance, facilitating Gaza's reconstruction, and
advancing a credible political pathway towards Palestinian self-
determination and statehood.
[page 2]
Unfortunately, however, the situation on the ground remains deeply
concerning, underscoring the considerable distance still to be covered
between diplomatic commitments and their realization. Despite the
ceasefire, more than 1,600 Palestinians have been killed in Gaza due
to Israeli aggression. Although humanitarian conditions have
improved in certain respects, the scale of assistance remains grossly
inadequate, compared to the overwhelming needs of the population.
Widespread displacement, devastated infrastructure, and severe
shortages of essential services, continue to compound the suffering
of the Palestinian people.

The immediate imperative is to ensure full and faithful
implementation of the ceasefire, an end to all violations, and effective
protection of Palestinian civilians. Humanitarian assistance must
flow safely and without impediment. UNRWA must be fully enabled
to discharge its indispensable mandate. The complete withdrawal of
Israeli forces from Gaza and its comprehensive reconstruction
remain urged essentials. Equally, there must be full and credible
accountability for grave violations of international law and atrocities
committed against the Palestinian people.

Beyond Gaza, the relentless expansion of illegal Israeli settlements,
escalating settler violence, forced displacement, and systematic
demolition of Palestinian homes in the occupied West Bank,
including East Jerusalem, represents a grave assault on the rights of
the Palestinian people and the prospects for a Two-State Solution.
These unlawful policies and practices are an attempt to steadily erode
the territorial integrity and viability of a future Palestinian State and
must cease immediately. Pakistan unequivocally condemns the
repeated incursions into and desecration of Islamic and Christian
holy sites, particularly the blessed Al-Aqsa Mosque, and rejects all
attempts to alter their historical and legal status quo.

Pakistan remains steadfast in its commitment to a just,
comprehensive and lasting resolution of the Palestinian question and
will continue its efforts towards the establishment of an independent,
sovereign, viable and contiguous State of Palestine, on the basis of
[page 3]
the pre-1967 borders, with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as its capital, in
accordance with relevant United Nations resolutions and
international law.

On this solemn occasion, Pakistan reiterates its unwavering solidarity
with the Palestinian people. Their legitimate aspirations for freedom,
statehood, as well as justice, and dignity must no longer remain
unfulfilled. The time has come to turn the promise of peace into a
reality for the Palestinian people.
