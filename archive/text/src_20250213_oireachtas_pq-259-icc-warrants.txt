          <question by="#ConorDMcGuinness" to="#Justice" eId="pq_259">
            <p eId="para_2736">259. <b>Deputy Conor D. McGuinness</b> asked the <b>Minister for Justice</b> the provisions his Department or the bodies under his aegis has made for the identification, apprehension, and arrest of persons subject to arrest warrants issued by the International Criminal Court that may enter Ireland, or transit through Irish territorial waters or airspace. [5363/25]</p>
          </question>
          <speech by="#JimOCallaghan" as="#Minister_for_Justice" eId="spk_571">
            <from>Minister for Justice<recordedTime time="2025-02-13T18:16:00+00:00"/></from>
            <p eId="para_2737">Very close attention is paid to all unsealed arrest warrants issued by the International Criminal Court. As the Deputy will appreciate, operational details in that regard are not made public. </p>
            <p eId="para_2738"> If, in due course, Ireland were to receive a request for the arrest and surrender of a person who is subject to an International Criminal Court arrest warrant, such a request would fall to be dealt with in accordance with the International Criminal Court Act 2006 (as amended).</p>
