TEXT OF THE MESSAGE BY
HIS EXCELLENCY DR MOHAMED MUIZZU
PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF MALDIVES

QUOTE

Malé, 23 November 2026

The International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People reminds
us of the unimaginable suffering Palestinians continue to endure. The world
must act now to stop Israel’s genocide, end apartheid and illegal settlements,
protect Palestinian lives, ensure humanitarian assistance, and secure their right
to an independent and sovereign State of Palestine.

Since I assumed office three years ago, the Maldives has intensified its
advocacy for Palestinian rights at the United Nations and other multilateral
forums. In October 2024, the Maldives intervened in the case brought by South
Africa against Israel at the International Court of Justice. That same year,
Maldivians raised more than US$1.2 million in donations for UNRWA. The
Maldives also increased its annual contribution to UNRWA tenfold, from
US$5,000 to US$50,000 in 2024.

A cause close to the hearts of Maldivians is the safety, security and rights
of the Palestinian people. This includes upholding the ceasefire, ensuring
unimpeded access to essential humanitarian assistance, and bringing an end to
the ongoing violence. As reports of relentless, daily attacks in Palestine persist,
it is evident that the basic human right of safety continues to be denied. The
international community must ensure unimpeded access to humanitarian relief
for the people of Palestine, including medicine, food, water, and shelter.

In this context, the Maldives reaffirms its unwavering support for
UNRWA, whose mandate remains indispensable for the survival of the
Palestinian people. The Maldives calls on the international community to
provide UNRWA with the political and financial support necessary to fulfil its
mandate.

The Maldives recognizes the importance of equitable participation in
global decision-making. While the State of Palestine maintains its voice at the
United Nations, true representation requires complete voting rights. The State
of Palestine must be granted full participation and equal rights in all UN bodies,
as a full member of the United Nations.

The Maldives reaffirms its continued commitment to and unconditional
support for the Palestinian people in their pursuit of their inalienable rights,
including the right to self-determination. The Maldives calls for universal
recognition of an independent and sovereign State of Palestine, based on the
pre-1967 borders, with East Jerusalem as its capital.

Dignity and safety are fundamental human rights, not privileges. The
Maldives calls for an end to violence and a peaceful resolution achieved
through meaningful diplomacy. Let us move from declaration to action.

Dr Mohamed Muizzu
President of the Republic of Maldives
