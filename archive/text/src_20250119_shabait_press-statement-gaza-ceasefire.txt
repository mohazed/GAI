GeneralPress Release
Last updated Jan 19, 2025
Share
The Gaza Ceasefire Agreement is scheduled for implementation today.
The people and Government of Eritrea reaffirm, what is not novel but indeed their historical solidarity, with the Palestinian people. Congratulations Palestine!
Once again in the 21st century, the people of Palestine have demonstrated, through unparalleled sacrifices and resilience, that the inalienable rights of peoples to liberation and independence cannot be vanquished by force and coercion. As such, the people and Government of Eritrea urge all peace-loving peoples to extend their support to the crystallization of the ultimate aspirations of the people of Palestine for an independent Palestinian State.
Ministry of Information
Asmara
19 January 2025
