Statement By H.E. Retno L.P. Marsudi Minister for Foreign Affairs  of the Republic of Indonesia  At the 15th Session Of The Islamic Summit Conference “Enhancing Unity And Solidarity Through Dialogue For Sustainable Development” Banjul, 4 May 2024
Bismillahirrahmanirrahim,
Excellencies,
Thank you, Gambia, for hosting the OIC Summit.
I would like to focus on TWO critical issues. First, the Palestinian issue. Second, the OIC development agenda and women's issue. Let me start with the Palestinian issue.
​Bismillahirrahmanirrahim,
Excellencies,
Thank you, Gambia, for hosting the OIC Summit.
I would like to focus on TWO critical issues. First, the Palestinian issue. Second, the OIC development agenda and women's issue. Let me start with the Palestinian issue.
Colleagues,
We all understand that this issue is at the very core of OIC. The OIC owes independence to the Palestinian people. I would like to remind us of the Arab Peace Initiative and the OIC decision that peace with Israel will only be possible if Israel ends its occupation of Palestine.
That decision sent a strong message to Israel: without independence for Palestine, there can be no diplomatic relations. That message and decision should be maintained.
Colleagues,
Over the last 7 months, we have witnessed the worst atrocities in modern history. More than 34 thousand Palestinians were killed by Israel which is a genocide. Humanitarian assistance is always hindered. Threats to attack Rafah continue. Palestine's UN membership is continuously blocked.
In this difficult situation, the OIC must stand united to defend justice and humanity for the Palestinians.
First, do not let our attention be divided.
Our focus should remain united on helping Palestine. We must sustain humanitarian assistance for people in Gaza by any means necessary to continue our support for UNRWA. Therefore, the agreed Islamic Financial Safety Net in OIC must be activated immediately.
Second, keep pushing for an immediate and permanent ceasefire.
A ceasefire would be a game-changer to stop the rising casualties and ease humanitarian suffering. It's also crucial for creating a conducive environment for fair negotiations towards a two-state solution.
Third, prevent further escalation.
We need to focus on addressing humanitarian disaster in Palestine and restrain ourselves from open conflict. We must ensure the stability of the region and the world. The unity of OIC must contribute to peace, not further the crisis.
Second point I would like to mention is on the OIC agenda of development and women's issue
Colleagues,
OIC faces a significant challenge in narrowing the development gap. Some member countries are grappling with widespread poverty by which 21 out of 46 LDCs are OIC members.
Indonesia urges deeper private sector engagement in the OIC through fostering more trade deals and investments. In this context, Indonesia will host the 2nd Indonesia - Africa Forum this September in Bali.
Women empowerment and access to quality education are also crucial for inclusive OIC development. This should be a priority for OIC, including to promote women's education rights in Afghanistan.
Women in Afghanistan and other parts of the world deserve equal rights and the issue of women should be mainstreamed in the policies and activities of the OIC.
Indonesia has offered scholarships for Afghan women and girls, shared best practices on madrassa curricula with the UNAMA, and delivered 10 million doses of polio vaccine for Afghan children.
Indonesia calls for the OIC to take more actions for a broader impact in Afghanistan
Excellencies,
The challenges we face are unprecedented. However, by nurturing unity and solidarity among us, we will solve those challenges for Palestine and all OIC member countries.
Thank you.​
Source: Ministry of Foreign Affairs
