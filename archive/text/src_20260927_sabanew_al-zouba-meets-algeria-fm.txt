Foreign Minister Discusses with Her Algerian Counterpart Bilateral Cooperation
[27/09/2026 06:11]
NEW YORK - SABA
The Minister of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates Dr. Afrah al-Zouba discussed today, Sunday, with the State Minister, Minister of Foreign Affairs, National Community Abroad, and African Affairs of the People's Democratic Republic of Algeria Ahmed Attaf the bilateral relations and cooperation between the two countries.
The talks took place on the sidelines of the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly.
The conversations covered the developments in Yemen and their humanitarian, economic, and security repercussions, particularly concerning navigation security in the Red Sea.
Al-Zouba expressed Yemen's appreciation for Algeria's firm stances in supporting Arab causes and backing Yemen in various forums.
Ataf confirmed Algeria's supportive position towards Yemen, respecting its constitutional legitimacy, unity, sovereignty, independence and territorial integrity, and supporting efforts aimed at achieving security, stability and peace.
