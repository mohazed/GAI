Sudan: Ambassador presents copy of credentials
11 October 2026
Yaqoub Alruqaishi presented a copy of his credentials as Ambassador of the Sultanate of Oman to the Republic of Sudan during a meeting with Mohieddin Salem Ahmed, Sudan’s Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation.
The meeting reviewed relations between the two countries and discussed ways to develop cooperation in various fields to serve the mutual interests of the Omani and Sudanese peoples.
The Ambassador expressed his commitment to advancing the relations between the two countries, while the Minister welcomed him and wished him success in carrying out his duties.
