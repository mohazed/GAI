Foreign Minister Bids Farewell to Outgoing Saudi Ambassador
Mogadishu (MFA Somalia) – The Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, H.E. Abdisalam Ali, today received the outgoing Ambassador of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to Somalia, H.E. Mr. Ahmed Mohammed Saleh Al-Muwalad, for a farewell meeting.
The Minister thanked the Ambassador for his role in strengthening the brotherly ties between the two nations and wished him success in his next assignment.
