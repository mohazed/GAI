Reference: C.N.263.2026.TREATIES-XVIII.10 (Depositary Notification)
ROME STATUTE OF THE INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL COURT
ROME, 17 JULY 1998
BURKINA FASO: WITHDRAWAL1
The Secretary-General of the United Nations, acting in his capacity as depositary,
communicates the following:
The above action was effected on 24 June 2026, with:
(Translation) (Original: French)
…
When it acceded to the Rome Statute, Burkina Faso nurtured the hope of contributing to the
establishment of an international order based on justice, equity and the fight against impunity. At the
time, the International Criminal Court appeared to be an impartial instrument dedicated to peace, human
dignity and respect for fundamental rights, inspiring great hope, in particular among the peoples of
Africa.
However, it must be acknowledged that, over time, the Court gradually moved away from its
founding ideals, becoming a selective and politicized tool, to the detriment of the impartiality and
objectivity that were expected to define it.
…
***
The action shall take effect for Burkina Faso on 24 June 2027 in accordance with
article 127 (1) which reads as follows:
“A State Party may, by written notification addressed to the Secretary-General of the
United Nations, withdraw from this Statute. The withdrawal shall take effect one year after the date of
receipt of the notification, unless the notification specifies a later date.”
30 June 2026
