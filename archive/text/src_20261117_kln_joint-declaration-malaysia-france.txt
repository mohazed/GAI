JOINT DECLARATION ON THE STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP
BETWEEN MALAYSIA AND THE FRENCH REPUBLIC
17 NOVEMBER 2026
1. The Honourable Dato’ Seri Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister of Malaysia and His
Excellency Emmanuel Macron, President of the French Republic held a Bilateral
Meeting on 17 November 2026 in Kuala Lumpur during the President’s Official Visit
to Malaysia.
2. On this occasion, Prime Minister Anwar and President Macron announced the
elevation of bilateral relations to a Strategic Partnership. This marks a historic
advancement in Malaysia-France relations, building upon a foundation of diplomatic
engagement since Malaysia’s independence in 1957. This Strategic Partnership is a
testament to the enduring depth of cooperation across strategic areas, particularly in
trade and investments, defence and security, culture, science, technology and
innovation, education and research. The initiatives led by the two countries in those
key sectors contribute to both Malaysia’s and France’s resilience and sovereignty.
3. Both leaders affirmed that active Bilateral Consultations provide a necessary impetus
for aligning strategic interests of Malaysia and France amid an increasingly complex
geopolitical landscape. They agreed this mechanism is the linchpin for reviewing
bilateral progress, charting future cooperation and ensuring the effective
implementation of the Strategic Partnership to address shared regional and global
challenges.
Pillars of Strategic Partnership
Pillar 1: Partnership in Sustainable Economic and Emerging Technologies
4. Prime Minister Anwar and President Macron recognised the importance of
sustainable economic partnerships for the future development of both countries. To
that end, the two leaders welcomed the launch of the First High-Level Business
Leaders’ Council between France and Malaysia, with a view to strengthening the
direct ties between the two private sectors. Both leaders agreed to deepen
collaboration in areas of emerging technologies relying on critical minerals and
energy transition, such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), semiconductors, digital
innovation, undersea cables, advanced manufacturing, renewable energy and civil
nuclear power.
5. Both leaders welcomed the signing of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on
Cooperation in the Field of Critical Minerals that will foster technological transfer and
sharing of expertise as well as ensure access to critical minerals to projects mutually
beneficial to Malaysia and France, leading towards a sustainable partnership and
synergies in research and development between the two countries. They also
welcomed the signing of the MoU on Cooperation in the Field of Energy Transition,
focusing on renewable energy and energy efficiency, nuclear energy for the purpose
of generating electricity, enhancing power grid infrastructure for regional energy
integration and investment as well as financing mechanisms in the field of energy.

6. Both leaders further welcomed the positive momentum in the ongoing negotiation of
the Malaysia-European Union Free Trade Agreement (MEUFTA) that seeks to
enhance market access, facilitate trade and investment flow, strengthen industrial
and supply chain linkages, support sustainable development and create new
opportunities for businesses in Malaysia and European Union (EU). Prime Minister
Anwar and President Macron agreed that this instrument is a gateway for Malaysian
enterprises seeking to expand their presence in the European market, while
reaffirming Malaysia’s position as a regional hub for businesses from France and the
rest of Europe seeking to strengthen their engagement with Malaysia and the wider
ASEAN market. Malaysia and France will also work together to contribute to the
decarbonization and energy security of ASEAN and strengthen regional
interconnectivity to support the realization of the ASEAN Power Grid. They welcomed
the future launch of an ASEAN regional school on energy regulation in Malaysia, with
the support of the Agence Française de Développement Group (AFD Group).
Pillar 2: Partnership in Defence, Security and Crisis Management
7. Prime Minister Anwar and President Macron recognised the significant and
longstanding defence ties between Malaysia and France, including through regular
exchanges and cooperation within the framework of the Malaysia-France Defence
Joint High Strategic Committee (DJHSC). Both leaders reaffirmed their commitment
to strengthening Malaysia-France defence and security relations through capability
development and closer defence industry partnerships including the development of
defence technologies and industrial collaboration to support Malaysia’s defence
modernisation and capabilities.
8. The signing of the Roadmap on Bilateral Defence Capabilities and Defence Industrial
Partnership will further enhance and strengthen Malaysian and French defence
ecosystems through cooperation in defence capabilities and industrial partnerships.
The two parties welcome the initial implementation of the roadmap, materialized by
two concrete projects: the selection of Malaysia for the H225M Combat Search and
Rescue helicopters and the 105LG cannons.
9. The two leaders encouraged the continuation of stopovers, visits and joint exercised
by and between French and Malaysian military assets, as well as training of officials.
They look forward to the conclusion of the negotiation on the Status of Forces
Agreement (SOFA) and its signing in the foreseeable future.
10. The two leaders also welcomed the signing of the amendment to the security
agreement on the exchange and protection of information classified in the field of
defence (ASDD), which facilitates deeper exchanges between Malaysia and France
and offers new avenues for defence cooperation.
11. In addition, the setting up of the Regional Centre for Security Against Hybrid
Interference (RC-SHIELD) will reinforce both countries’ preparedness and resilience
against hybrid threats and grey-zones activities.
12. Malaysia being the host of ASEANAPOL Secretariat and ASEAN Coordinating
Centre for Humanitarian Assistance on Disaster Management (AHA Centre), Prime
Minister Anwar expressed appreciation to President Macron for France’s valuable
engagement with ASEAN through experience-sharing and technical expertise

development in combating transnational crime and managing crises in this region.
This cooperation reflects a shared commitment in addressing security challenges by
supporting ASEAN Centrality and upholding the principles of the United Nations
Charter.
Pillar 3: Partnership in Education, Skill Enhancement and People-to-people
Connectivity
13. Prime Minister Anwar and President Macron recognised the strategic importance of
education and human capital development in navigating the evolving knowledge-
driven ecosystem and technology-oriented economy. Both leaders welcomed the
signing of the MoU on Cooperation in the Field of Higher Education, as an important
milestone in cementing bilateral education ties and fostering innovation.
14. Both leaders encouraged further collaboration between institutions of higher learning
through academic and faculty exchanges, joint research and partnerships in
emerging and strategic fields, including science, technology, engineering and
mathematics (STEM), AI, digital technologies and sustainable development. They
welcomed initiatives to strengthen public-private partnership in financing and
accelerating research and development in advanced technologies. They also
encouraged synergies between governments, industries, universities and research
institutions to mobilise resources, translate scientific researches into practical
applications and promote innovation, thereby enhancing the technological
capabilities and global competitiveness of both countries. Both leaders also
reaffirmed their commitment to expanding cooperation through joint programmes for
teachers and students in the field of technical and vocational education and training
(TVET) which has become increasingly important in supplementing the
abovementioned emerging and strategic fields.
15. Both leaders underlined the success of the Malaysian flagship scholarship program
under strategic institutions such as the Ministry of Education, Public Service
Department and Council of Trust for the People (Majlis Amanah Rakyat – MARA),
and has agreed to further develop cooperation through joint scholarships to expand
student mobility. Both leaders welcomed a possible relaunch of the Hubert Curien
program, which facilitates joint research projects.
16. Both leaders underscored the vital importance of deepening people-to-people
connectivity, cultural exchanges and tourism cooperation to foster mutual
understanding and lasting goodwill between the two nations. They agreed to expand
partnerships across the arts, heritage preservation and museum development
through institutional linkages, government-to-government mechanisms and
multilateral platforms such as UNESCO. In this regard, both leaders welcomed
closer collaboration among leading cultural institutions and heritage sites, focusing
on curatorial exchanges, joint exhibitions, conservation and professional capacity-
building.
17. Prime Minister Anwar and President Macron recognised people-to-people
connectivity serves as a natural bridge between communities and reaffirmed their
commitment to promoting two-way tourism and cultural diplomacy. They expressed

confidence that these joint efforts will celebrate both countries’ rich and diverse
heritages, forging stronger grassroots ties.
Advancing Multilateralism in Addressing Global Challenges
18. France and Malaysia are committed to working on building new partnership of
independents and sovereign nations between the EU and Southeast Asia in order to
preserve peace, security and prosperity. Both countries share a firm commitment to
multilateralism and will work together to uphold the fundamental principles of the
United Nations Charter, including territorial integrity, international law and the rules-
based international order. They also expressed their determination to strengthen
Malaysia-France engagement in multilateral fora and to collaborate with the
international community in advancing common interests and delivering practical
solutions to global challenges.
19. Both leaders emphasised the essential role of the United Nations in the international
architecture while acknowledging the necessity of its institutional reform, particularly
the United Nations Security Council (UNSC). Prime Minister Anwar views France’s
position as one of the Permanent Members to be instrumental in moving the reform
forward. They supported the UN80 initiative launched by the UN Secretary-General.
Malaysia supports the joint initiative by France and Mexico to refrain use of the veto
in the UNSC in cases of mass atrocities.
20. Both leaders expressed deep concern over the grave situation in West Asia, and
called on all parties to de-escalate, exercise restraint, and pursue a diplomatic
resolution to the ongoing conflicts, particularly regarding the situation in Gaza,
Jerusalem and the West Bank including the increasing violence of settlers and the
expansion of illegal settlement in the Occupied Palestinian Territory. They reiterated
their commitment to the two-state solution and called for a full and unhindered
access of humanitarian aid into Gaza and the Occupied Palestinian Territory. They
also reiterated their support for the New York Declaration and the UNSCR 2803
(2025).
21. Prime Minister Anwar commended France, together with the United Kingdom,
Canada and other countries, for the decision to ban the importation of goods from
Israel’s illegal settlements and other measures against Israel. Both leaders also
called for the full and effective implementation of the 26 June 2026 ceasefire
framework agreement to allow for robust and lasting peace and security in Lebanon,
whose sovereignty and territorial integrity must be preserved.
22. Both leaders called on relevant parties to agree swiftly on a full and immediate
ceasefire with a view to negotiating a comprehensive, just and lasting peace between
Ukraine and the Russian Federation, in accordance with international law and based
on the fundamental principles of the UN Charter.
23. Both leaders also reaffirmed the central importance of ASEAN and the EU in
promoting global stability and prosperity. They expressed their commitment to
strengthening cooperation across all areas of ASEAN-EU Dialogue Relations and
their support to the EU's intention to establish an ASEAN-EU Comprehensive
Strategic Partnership (CSP), with the view to the ASEAN-EU Commemorative
Summit to be held in 2027. Prime Minister Anwar welcomed France’s continued

engagement with ASEAN through ASEAN Defense Ministers Meeting Plus and its
application to be an ASEAN Sectoral Dialogue Partner. The Prime Minister
expressed readiness to consider France’s application favourably, in accordance with
ASEAN’s established processes and procedures.
24. Both leaders recognised the role of ASEAN in achieving a comprehensive and lasting
peace in Myanmar through inclusive dialogues involving all stakeholders with the
Five-Point Consensus serving as the main reference. They reiterate the urgent
necessity of the immediate cessation of all violence, without conditions, and call for
the establishment of a safe and conducive environment in ensuring the delivery of
humanitarian aid and assistance, to reach those in needs.
25. Both leaders stressed the need of an open and inclusive trade architecture that
emphasised the important role of the World Trade Organisation (WTO) in
strengthening the rules-based multilateral trading system and in countering
protectionism. They also supported the strengthening of the relations between the
EU and the CPTPP.
26. Prime Minister Anwar and President Macron reaffirmed the importance of
maintaining and promoting peace, stability, safety and freedom of navigation, in line
with the 1982 UN Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS), including in the
South China Sea. Both leaders reaffirmed their commitment to the principle of
peaceful resolution of disputes in accordance with international law, including 1982
UNCLOS.
27. Both leaders reaffirmed the importance of maintaining safe and unimpeded maritime
navigation through the Strait of Hormuz, in accordance with international law and
emphasised the importance of minimising disruptions to international trade flows and
global supply chains. President Macron underlined the importance of the French-
British initiative on freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz. He also recognised
the critical role and contribution of Malaysia, along with the other coastal states, in
promoting an unimpeded maritime navigation and trade through the Strait of
Malacca, in accordance with international law and the respective sovereignty and
jurisdiction of the coastal States.
28. Stressing the importance of sustainable blue economy and maritime security for
development and stability, both leaders agreed to explore the development of a joint
road map to further cooperation in these areas.
29. Addressing the triple planetary crisis, the two leaders reaffirmed their commitments
to the Paris Agreement's 1.5°C goal and the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity
Framework, while welcoming the entry into force of the Biodiversity Beyond National
Jurisdiction (BBNJ) Agreement. They committed to marine conservation, ending
plastic pollution, accelerating global energy transitions away from coal and
advancing high-integrity carbon markets to ensure sustainable environmental
stewardship for future generations.
30. The Official Visit of President Macron to Malaysia solidified the shared commitment
of both countries to bolstering economic security and prosperity and deepening
strategic ties. The visit officially elevates the bilateral relations between Malaysia and
France to a Strategic Partnership.
