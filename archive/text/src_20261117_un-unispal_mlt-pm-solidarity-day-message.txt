PRIME MINISTER
AUBERGE DE CASTILLE, VALLETTA, MALTA

17 November 2026

Your Excellency,

On the occasion of the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People, I wish to reaffirm
Malta’s enduring solidarity with the Palestinian people and our support for their legitimate aspirations
to live in freedom, dignity and security.

At a time when the region continues to face considerable uncertainty, this day reminds us not only of
the suffering endured by generations of Palestinians, but also of our collective responsibility to work
towards a future in which peace and statehood can finally be realised.

Against this backdrop, developments over the past year have reinforced the need for a credible political
horizon, guided by diplomacy, dialogue and respect for international law, towards a comprehensive, just
and lasting peace.

Malta supports efforts to consolidate a cessation of hostilities in Gaza, and enable a transition to
sustained stability, recovery and a genuine political process. We reiterate that Hamas should not have
any role in the future governance of Gaza.

Alongside these political efforts, the deeply concerning humanitarian situation requires the rapid, safe
and unhindered delivery of humanitarian assistance and essential services to those in need. The
international community must remain sensitive to the urgent needs of civilians, while supporting the
conditions that allow Palestinian families to rebuild their lives in dignity.

Malta’s recognition of the State of Palestine in September 2025 formalised a position that we have
upheld consistently for decades. Our responsibility now is to build upon that decision by supporting the
Palestinian Authority as it advances its reform agenda and by strengthening our bilateral relations with
Palestine through cooperation and capacity building.

In this regard, we call for credible, free and inclusive Palestinian elections, regrettably postponed,
through which the Palestinian people can determine their leadership and shape their State’s future.
Renewed democratic legitimacy is essential to strengthening accountable Palestinian institutions.

At the same time, developments in the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, remain a matter of serious
concern. Settlement expansion, settler violence, forced displacement and other measures that further
undermine the territorial contiguity of a future Palestinian State risk eroding the prospects of the very
peace we seek to achieve.
[page 2]
In light of these concerns, I reiterate that Malta remains unequivocally opposed to actions that violate
international law and undermine prospects for peace and will continue to underscore the importance of
preserving the viability of the Two-State Solution.

Our vision remains clear: Israel and Palestine living side by side in peace and security, within mutually
recognised borders, in accordance with the relevant United Nations resolutions and internationally
agreed parameters. The Two-State-Solution remains the only viable path capable of addressing the
legitimate aspirations of both peoples. Achieving this vision will require leadership and sustained
international engagement. It will also require strong and accountable Palestinian institutions capable
of contributing to lasting peace and stability.

Excellency, solidarity must ultimately be measured not only through words, but through our collective
determination to translate peace frameworks into concrete action.

Malta will continue to play its part, within the United Nations and the European Union, and through our
bilateral relations. In doing so, we will continue to support the Palestinian people and work towards a
future in which Palestinians and Israelis alike may live in dignity, security and peace. This remains the
surest foundation for lasting stability in the Middle East.

Please accept, Excellency, the assurances of my highest consideration.

[signature]

Robert Abela
Prime Minister

H.E Coly Seck

Chair of the United Nations Committee on the Exercise of the Inalienable Rights of the
