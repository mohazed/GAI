No. 716
The Permanent Mission of the State of Libya to the United Nations presents its compliments to the
Secretariat of the Committee on the Exercise of the Inalienable Rights of the Palestinian People and, with
reference to the letter of the Chair of the Committee dated 27 October 2026 concerning the observance of
the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People on Monday, 23 November 2026, has the
honour to transmit herewith the message of solidarity from H.E. Mr. Eltaher Salem Elbaour, Acting Minister
of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the State of Libya, addressed to H.E. Mr. Coly Seck,
Chair of the Committee, on this occasion.
In this regard, the Permanent Mission would be grateful if the said message could be included in the
list of messages to be read out by the Chair at the special commemorative meeting of the Committee, and
posted, in its original Arabic language, on the website of the Committee.
A copy of the said message is attached herewith.
The Permanent Mission of the State of Libya to the United Nations avails itself of this opportunity to
renew to the Secretariat of the Committee on the Exercise of the Inalienable Rights of the Palestinian People
the assurances of its highest consideration.
New York, 19 November 2026
Secretariat of the Committee on the Exercise of the Inalienable Rights of the Palestinian People
New York
