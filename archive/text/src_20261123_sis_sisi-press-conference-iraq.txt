“In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful”
My brother, Ali al-Zaidi,
Prime Minister of the Republic of Iraq,
Ladies and gentlemen,
Allow me to welcome you, my dear brother the Prime Minister, on your first official visit to your second home, Egypt.
I would like to take this opportunity to extend, once again, my congratulations on the assumption of your post, and underscore the profound and enduring relations between Egypt and Iraq, as well as our keen interest in advancing the historical bonds between our two brotherly nations.
I wish to express our pride in Egypt’s hosting of the fourth session of the Egyptian-Iraqi Joint Higher Committee, co-chaired by the Prime Ministers of both countries.
The session comes at a juncture marked by growing momentum in our political relations and economic cooperation. In this regard, I commend the periodic and rotational convening of this committee between Egypt and Iraq, which reflects our shared determination to elevate bilateral relations across all domains.
Ladies and gentlemen,
Today, I held constructive talks with His Excellency the Prime Minister, addressing an array of issues of mutual interest.
These discussions reaffirmed our resolve to translate the understandings we have agreed upon into tangible steps fulfilling the aspirations of our two brotherly peoples for progress, prosperity, and development.
We also agreed to strengthen mechanisms of political consultation, particularly in light of the compounding challenges facing the region.
Our discussions covered a range of regional and international issues; we exchanged views on ways to preserve regional security and stability, and to devise approaches conducive to defusing crises and de-escalating tensions across the region.
There was also an alignment of views with regard to the necessity to strengthen mechanisms of joint Arab action, with the aim of safeguarding Arab national security and protecting the unity, sovereignty, and resources of Arab states.
In this regard, I reiterate Egypt’s unshakable support for the sovereignty, unity, and stability of brotherly Iraq, as well as the preservation of the gains it has achieved over the past years.
Ladies and Gentlemen,
We also addressed developments regarding the Palestinian cause, affirming the vital importance of upholding the ceasefire and ensuring Israel’s fulfilment of its obligations stipulated in the first phase of President Trump’s peace plan, including the entry of humanitarian aid and early recovery supplies, as well as the rehabilitation of infrastructure and hospitals in the Gaza Strip.
I further stressed the critical need to implement the requirements of the second phase, including completing the Israeli withdrawal from the Gaza Strip, empowering the National Committee for the Administration of Gaza to exercise its interim mandate from within the Strip, and deploying the International Stabilization Force to monitor the ceasefire.
This shall culminate in enabling the brotherly Palestinian people to exercise their right to self-determination and establish their Independent State along the June 4, 1967 borders, with East Jerusalem as its capital, in accordance with the resolutions of international legitimacy.
Our discussions also touched upon the situation in Yemen. We underscored the necessity to support regional and international efforts aimed at resuming the path of a comprehensive and sustainable political settlement to the crisis in Yemen, in a manner that ensures its unity and stability.
We also concurred on the crucial importance of pursuing diplomatic efforts to preserve the security, safety, and freedom of maritime navigation in accordance with the principles of international law.
We reaffirmed our categorical rejection of any attempts to undermine the freedom and security of navigation in the Arabian Gulf, the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, and the Red Sea, given the threat this poses to regional and international security and stability.
I reaffirmed that the security and governance of the Red Sea are the sole and exclusive prerogative of the littoral Arab and African states, to the absolute exclusion of all others.
Our talks also addressed the issue of water security, particularly as both of our countries are downstream states. We stressed the significance of bolstering coordination and frameworks of cooperation in confronting the challenges of water scarcity and desertification.
We stressed the imperative of adhering to international law governing transboundary watercourses, rejecting unilateral measures, and committing to cooperation through prior notification, consultation, and consensus to deliver mutual benefit for all parties.
My brother, Prime Minister,
Once again, I welcome you to Egypt, looking forward to achieving even closer cooperation between our two countries, in a manner that fulfills the aspirations of our two brotherly peoples and our Arab nation. I wish brotherly Iraq enduring prosperity and well-being.
