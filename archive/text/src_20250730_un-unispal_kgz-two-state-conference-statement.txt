Check against delivery
Statement
by the Deputy Permanent Representative of the Kyrgyz Republic to
the UN Mr. Meder Utebaev at the High-level International
Conference for the Peaceful Settlement of the Question of Palestine
and the Implementation of the Two-State Solution.
(30 July 2025)
Distinguished Co-Chairs,
Excellencies,
Let me also thank to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the France
for convening this important conference.
We also commend contributions and ongoing engagement of all
high-level representatives presented over the past three days, as we
collectively strive to address the tragic situation in Palestine.
Kyrgyzstan stands in full solidarity with the people of Palestine,
particularly in light of the devastating humanitarian situation in Gaza.
We are gravely concerned by the suffering endured by innocent
civilians—especially women, children, and the elderly.
The agreement from 19 January 2025 brought a brief yet significant
pause in hostilities, enabled the exchange of prisoners and hostages, and
allowed critical humanitarian assistance—delivered by the United Nations,
the ICRC, and other partners—to reach those in urgent need.
Regrettably, this fragile ceasefire was short-lived. Hostilities have
resumed, and once again, it is the civilian population that bears the
greatest burden.
However, the situation is not hopeless, let’s collectively reaffirm our
unwavering commitment to peace, dialogue, and the protection of human
life.
We call all parties to exercise maximum restraint and to uphold their
responsibilities under international humanitarian law, especially with
regard to the civilian population of Gaza.
Excellencies,

Today, 149 United Nations Member States have recognized the
State of Palestine.
Kyrgyzstan is proud to be among them. We established diplomatic
relations with Palestine back in 1995, and this year we will celebrate the
30th anniversary of our diplomatic ties.
We welcome the decision made by France on the recognition of
State of Palestine, and the decision of the United Kingdom, which was
announced yesterday at this very podium.
We believe that a just, lasting, and comprehensive solution to the
Israeli-Palestinian conflict must be based on the relevant UN resolutions,
and the long-standing principle of two States for two people, living side by
side in peace, security, and mutual recognition.
Ladies and gentlemen,
The global community must remain united in its efforts to end the
bloodshed, to prevent further loss of innocent lives, prevent further
escalation of a full-scale humanitarian catastrophe.
We welcome the joint initiative of Saudi Arabia to establish an
international coalition to advance the implementation of the Two-State
solution.
Kyrgyzstan is ready and will contribute to all international efforts
aimed at achieving a just, comprehensive, and sustainable peace in the
Middle East.
I thank you.
