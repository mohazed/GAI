Joint Statement on the Official Visit of H.E. the President of the Arab Republic of Egypt to the Republic of Indonesia
Jakarta, October 2026
At the invitation of H.E. Prabowo Subianto, President of the Republic of Indonesia, H.E. Abdel Fattah El-Sisi, President of the Arab Republic of Egypt, paid an Official Visit to Indonesia on 10–11 October 2026, following President Prabowo's visits to Egypt in December 2024, April 2025, and October 2025.
During the visit, President El-Sisi was accorded a ceremonial welcome at Istana Merdeka, followed by bilateral talks with President Prabowo Subianto and a state luncheon. The two Presidents exchanged views on bilateral, regional, and global issues of mutual interest and witnessed the exchange of bilateral documents to advance the Indonesia–Egypt Strategic Partnership.
The two Presidents reaffirmed their commitment to further strengthening political cooperation and coordination through regular high-level exchanges and established bilateral mechanisms, including the Joint Commission Meeting (JCM). In this regard, the Plan of Action on the Strategic Partnership between the Arab Republic of Egypt and the Republic of Indonesia, agreed upon during the visit, underscores the two countries’ commitment to advancing the implementation of their Strategic Partnership.
The two Presidents welcomed the solid defence and security relations built on mutual cooperation and agreed to deepen and expand their scope, in a manner that enhances the national capabilities of both countries.
The two Presidents reaffirmed their commitment to deepening bilateral trade and economic cooperation by harnessing the significant potential and opportunities in both countries, expanding market access, facilitating business engagement and investment, enhancing the effectiveness of the Joint Trade Committee (JTC), and pursuing joint manufacturing projects in key sectors.
The two Presidents welcomed the significant progress achieved in advancing strategic cooperation between Indonesia’s Danantara and Egypt’s Future of Egypt Authority for Sustainable Development, reflecting their shared commitment to strengthening bilateral economic and investment relations. They highlighted the promising opportunities for cooperation in the production and distribution of crude palm oil and its derivative products, as well as phosphate fertilizers and related raw materials, with a view to promoting mutually beneficial investments and enhancing trade between the two countries. Both Presidents encouraged the two institutions to build on the understandings reached, advance jointinvestment opportunities, and expand cooperation into other strategic sectors, including infrastructure, energy, logistic, and agribusiness.
Building on the longstanding historical and cultural ties between the peoples of Egypt and Indonesia, the two Presidents agreed to strengthen people-to-people ties through enhanced cooperation in education, culture, language, and youth, including academic and institutional partnerships, student and school exchanges, and the launch of a new partnership for capacity building and technical cooperation by introducing a framework for mutually agreed programs designed to leverage collective capacities and advanced development goals.
President Prabowo welcomed Al-Azhar University's decision in November 2025 to introduce Indonesian language instruction at the Faculty of Languages and Translation and encouraged further strengthening of cooperation in Arabic language education in Indonesia.
The two Presidents underscored the importance of ensuring the safety and well-being of each other's nationals and encouraged strengthened cooperation between relevant authorities on consular matters and assistance, in accordance with the national regulations.
In light of evolving geopolitical and global challenges, the two Presidents underscored the importance of greater strategic convergence and closer coordination between Indonesia and Egypt in multilateral and regional forums.
The two Presidents expressed grave concern over the continued violence and Israeli military operations in the Occupied Palestinian Territory; the Gaza Strip and the West Bank, including East Jerusalem. They condemned Israel's illegal settlement policies and activities and called for their immediate and complete cessation, in accordance with international law and relevant United Nations resolutions.
The two Presidents reaffirmed their unwavering support for the realization of an independent, sovereign, viable and contiguous State of Palestine, based on the pre-1967 lines, with East Jerusalem as its capital, based on the two-State solution, and in accordance with international law and relevant United Nations resolutions.
The two Presidents reiterated their categorical rejection of any attempts to forcibly displace or expel Palestinians from their homeland under any pretext. They reaffirmed their commitment to achieving a just, comprehensive and lasting peace, including through the full implementation of President Trump's Comprehensive Plan to End the Gaza Conflict. Indonesia expressed appreciation for Egypt's pivotal role in facilitating humanitarian assistance to Gaza and reaffirmed its commitment to providing humanitarian assistance to the Palestinian people.
On the situation in the Gulf region, the two Presidents expressed grave concern over the continuing escalation and expansion of hostilities and the serious implications for regional security and stability, as well as civilian safety, freedom of navigation, energy security, and the global economy. They called for an immediate de-escalation, and maximum restraint by all parties. They also reaffirmed the importance of respecting the sovereignty and territorial integrity of all States, upholding international law and the United Nations Charter, and resolving disputes through dialogue and diplomacy.
The two Presidents recognized the growing challenges posed by climate change and water scarcity, and their implications for water and food security, sustainable development, and regional stability. In this context, the Indonesian side takes note of the vital importance of the Nile River’s water security for Egypt, while both sides emphasized the importance of full adherence to the principles of international law governing transboundary watercourses, including not causing harm.
President El-Sisi conveyed his sincere appreciation to President Prabowo Subianto for the warm welcome and excellent hospitality extended to him and his delegation during the visit. Wishing the friendly people of Indonesia prosperity and progress.
