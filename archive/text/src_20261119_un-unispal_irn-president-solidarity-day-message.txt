In the Name of God, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful

29 November, the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People,
has been designated as a day for the international community to express its solidarity
with, support for and unity with the resistance and liberation struggles of the
oppressed yet steadfast Palestinian people, and to voice its abhorrence of the
systematic occupation of Palestinian land and the atrocities and crimes perpetrated
by the Israeli regime against the Palestinian people over the past 80 years. 29
November serves as a reminder of eight decades of occupation of Palestinian
territories by the usurping Zionist regime, the enduring pain and suffering of the
Palestinian people, and the continued oppression and injustice inflicted upon this
oppressed nation. On this occasion, I wish, on behalf of the people and Government
of the Islamic Republic of Iran, to reaffirm my unequivocal support for the struggle
of the Palestinian people and the realisation of their aspiration to establish an
independent Palestinian State.

This day also embodies the bitter and regrettable reality of the international
community's inaction and the failure of international political and legal institutions
to confront eight decades of occupation, tyranny and apartheid imposed upon the
sacred land of Palestine by the usurping Israeli regime. Over these 80 years, this
regime has surpassed ISIS and all other criminals and terrorists throughout history
in the perpetration of atrocities and the killing of children, pushing the boundaries
of what constitutes mass atrocity crimes beyond all previously known limits.

Today, the international community observes this occasion at a time when,
since 7 October 2023, the occupying Israeli regime has pursued a “strategy of new
apartheid” throughout the occupied Palestinian territories, making the deprivation of
the Palestinian people of their human, political and social rights the cornerstone of
its policies. In this context, we have witnessed the implementation of scorched-earth
tactics, genocide and the killing of more than 75,000 innocent people in Gaza; the
continuation of policies of ethnic cleansing; the occupation and flattening of 70 per
cent of Gaza; the displacement of more than two million people and their forced
relocation to an area comprising less than one third of Gaza; the obstruction of the
entry of essential supplies and humanitarian assistance into Gaza; and the
deterioration of the humanitarian situation, particularly in the face of the current
[page 2]

flooding and winter cold. In the West Bank, we are likewise witnessing the
rapid expansion of settlement construction, the imposition of sieges, the
confiscation and destruction of Palestinian lands and properties, and the
intensification of terrorist and violent acts by settlers against Palestinians. Nor
must we overlook the plight of approximately 10,000 Palestinian prisoners held in
Israel's appalling and secret prisons, a substantial proportion of whom are
subjected to administrative detention and exposed to various forms of
psychological and physical torture and systematic violations of their human rights.

The international community is now confronted with the most shameful
moral and humanitarian crisis of the present century in Palestine. It must, as a
matter of urgency, fulfil its moral, political and legal responsibilities to liberate the
Palestinian people from the shadow of occupation, colonialism and apartheid, and
to secure the realisation of their inalienable right to self-determination and return to
their ancestral homeland. The Islamic Republic of Iran, reaffirming its continued
comprehensive support for the cause of the oppressed Palestinian people and
paying tribute to the steadfastness and admirable, legitimate resistance of
Palestinian youth against the aggression and occupation of the Zionist regime,
calls upon the international community to confront the actions of this criminal
regime, which are in flagrant violation of international law and the
provisions of numerous international resolutions.

The Islamic Republic of Iran, reaffirming its unwavering commitment
to providing comprehensive and unequivocal support for the cause of the
Palestinian people, and paying tribute to their courage, steadfastness and legitimate
and lawful resistance against the Zionist aggressors and occupiers, remains
firmly convinced that the only path towards a just and comprehensive resolution
of the question of Palestine and the achievement of lasting peace and security in
the West Asian region lies in the complete termination of the occupation of
Palestine, the return of Palestinian refugees, the exercise by the Palestinian
people of their right to self-determination, and their establishment of an
independent Palestinian State. To this end, the Islamic Republic of Iran has
previously presented its democratic proposal, grounded in international law, for
the “holding of a comprehensive referendum” among the original inhabitants of
Palestine, including Muslims, Christians and Jews, with a view to giving effect
to the fundamental principle of the “right to self-determination”. This proposal
remains on the table as the only comprehensive, just and lasting solution.
[page 3]

The Islamic Republic of Iran, in expressing its full solidarity with
the Palestinian people, will continue to support the courageous resistance and
heroic defence of the proud Palestinian nation against the rogue, criminal and
aggressor Israeli regime until the occupation of Palestinian territories comes to an
end and the noble aspirations of the Palestinian people are realised.

In closing, I once again salute the resistance of the free people and youth of
Palestine, and pray for their victory and for the liberation of Palestine from the
yoke of occupation.

May the peace, mercy, and blessings of God be bestowed upon you

Masoud Pezeshkian
President of the Islamic Republic of Iran
