Comment by the MFA of Ukraine on the Comprehensive Plan of the President of the United States of America Donald Trump to End the Gaza Conflict
Comment by the MFA of Ukraine on the Comprehensive Plan of the President of the United States of America Donald Trump to End the Gaza Conflict
01 October 2025 12:03
Foreign Affairs Israel Palestine USA
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine welcomes the publication of the President Donald J. Trump’s Comprehensive Plan to End the Gaza Conflict.
Ukraine highly values the leadership role of the United States in efforts to restore peace and stability in the Middle East, and maintain international peace and security.
We consider this plan as an important contribution to the search for a just and sustainable settlement that should be based on the principles of international law and take into account the legitimate rights and aspirations of the people in Israel and Palestine.
We call on all parties to implement the plan in practice as soon as possible in order to end the war, overcome the humanitarian crisis, and save human lives. We are convinced that the implementation of the plan will help restore security for the entire region, based on respect for international law and the UN Charter.
