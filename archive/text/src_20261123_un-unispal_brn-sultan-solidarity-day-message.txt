MESSAGE OF
HIS MAJESTY THE SULTAN OF BRUNEI DARUSSALAM
ON THE OCCASION OF THE INTERNATIONAL DAY OF
SOLIDARITY WITH THE PALESTINIAN PEOPLE
23RD NOVEMBER 2026

“The Government and people of Brunei Darussalam join me in reaffirming our
unwavering solidarity with the Palestinian people and our steadfast support for their
inalienable right to self-determination. Nearly eight decades have passed since the
question of Palestine was first brought before the United Nations, yet the Palestinian
people continue to await the fulfilment of their legitimate aspirations for freedom, justice,
dignity and statehood.

On this International Day of Solidarity, we are reminded that lasting peace cannot
be achieved without justice, nor can stability endure where fundamental rights remain
unfulfilled. The continued pursuit of a just and comprehensive solution is therefore not
only a responsibility to the Palestinian people, but also a shared commitment to a more
peaceful, secure and prosperous future for the Middle East and beyond.

The ceasefire in Gaza offers hope, but that hope must be sustained through
effective implementation, the protection of civilians, safe, rapid and unhindered
humanitarian access; and sustained support for Gaza’s recovery and reconstruction. We
are equally concerned by the escalating settler violence, the expansion of illegal
settlements, and measures toward annexation in the West Bank that continue to
undermine the prospects for peace and threaten the viability of a future Palestinian State.
We call upon the Occupying Power to comply with international law, and end its unlawful
presence in the Occupied Palestinian Territory as rapidly as possible, in accordance with
the Advisory Opinion of the International Court of Justice of July 2024.

The purposes and principles of the Charter of the United Nations and international
law must be upheld consistently and impartially, without exception or double standards.
The question of Palestine remains a test of the credibility of the United Nations,
particularly the Security Council, and of our collective resolve to uphold justice,
accountability and the rule of law.

To this end, we commend the continued efforts of the Secretary-General of the
United Nations, as well as the indispensable work of the United Nations Relief and Works
Agency for Palestine Refugees in the Near East (UNRWA), together with other
humanitarian agencies assisting the Palestinian people. We honour those who have lost
their lives in service of others, and stress the need to protect humanitarian workers,
personnel serving under the United Nations flag, and civilian infrastructure.
[page 3]
-2-

We welcome the growing international recognition of the State of Palestine and
urge those that have yet to do so to take this important step. Such recognition affirms the
Palestinian people’s inherent right to statehood and advance a just, comprehensive and
lasting peace in the Middle East can only be achieved through the two-State solution, with
an independent and sovereign State of Palestine, based on pre-1967 borders with East
Jerusalem as its capital.

The Palestinian people have waited far too long for justice, peace and the fulfilment
of their legitimate national aspirations. On this International Day of Solidarity, we reaffirm
that their right to self-determination, statehood and dignity cannot be deferred indefinitely.
Brunei Darussalam remains steadfast in its support for the Palestinian people and their
legitimate aspirations for an independent and sovereign State of Palestine. It is our hope
that future generations will inherit a region defined not by conflict and division, but by
peace, coexistence and shared prosperity.”
