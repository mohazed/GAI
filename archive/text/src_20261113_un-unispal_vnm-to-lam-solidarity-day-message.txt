MESSAGE
To:
- H.E. António Guterres, Secretary-General of the United Nations,
- H.E. Dr. Khalilur Rahman, President of the United Nations General
Assembly,
- H.E. Coly Seck, Chair of the United Nations Committee on the Exercise
of the Inalienable Rights of the Palestinian People,
- H.E. Mahmoud Abbas, President of the State of Palestine,
On the occasion of the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian
People, observed on 29 November, I wish, on behalf of the State and people of
Viet Nam, to convey to Your Excellencies and to the Palestinian people our
warmest greetings of friendship and solidarity.
Having experienced war and the struggle for independence, the people of
Viet Nam understand the Palestinian people’s aspiration to live in peace and
dignity in an independent State. We deeply sympathize with the Palestinian
people over the losses and suffering they continue to endure amid ongoing
violence and unmet humanitarian needs. We extend our deepest condolences to
families mourning the loss of their loved ones, and our heartfelt sympathies to
those who have been injured and to families separated from their family members
or deprived of their livelihoods.
Throughout the Palestinian people’s struggle, Viet Nam recognized the
State of Palestine at an early stage, and is pleased to see an increasing number of
countries sharing and supporting the legitimate aspirations of the Palestinian
people. Viet Nam consistently supports the just cause of the Palestinian people in
their struggle for independence, freedom and justice, and advocates a peaceful,
comprehensive, just and lasting settlement of the Question of Palestine, in
accordance with international law and relevant United Nations resolutions. We
reaffirm our steadfast support for the inalienable rights of the Palestinian people,
including their right to self-determination, and for a two-State solution, with an
independent and sovereign State of Palestine, living side by side in peace and
security with the State of Israel.
Viet Nam calls on all parties concerned to uphold the ceasefire in the Gaza
Strip, end all acts of hostility and work towards lasting peace. We stress the need
to respect international law, particularly international humanitarian law; to protect
civilians, humanitarian personnel and civilian infrastructure in the Gaza Strip and
the West Bank; and to refrain from actions that undermine the prospects for a two-
State solution. Safe, adequate, timely and unimpeded humanitarian access and
assistance must be ensured. At the same time, efforts to support recovery and

Viet Nam highly appreciates and supports the efforts of the United Nations
Secretary-General, United Nations agencies, countries and international partners
to end the violence, provide humanitarian assistance and advance the political
process towards a sustainable peace. We underscore the vital role of the United
Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees in the Near East
(UNRWA) and call on the international community to provide the Agency with
stable and sustainable funding. All parties concerned must ensure the safety of
UNRWA personnel and facilities and enable the Agency to fulfil its mandate to
assist Palestine refugees.
As a close and loyal friend, Viet Nam will continue to stand with the State
and people of Palestine. In that spirit, Viet Nam participates in the Board of Peace
(BoP) and works with United Nations agencies to make appropriate contributions
to helping the Palestinian people rebuild their lives and advancing lasting peace
in the region.
We are confident that, through the resilience of the Palestinian people and
the concerted efforts of the international community, future generations of
Palestinians will be able to live in peace, independence, security and prosperity.
Please accept, Your Excellencies, the assurances of my highest
consideration.
