President of Sri Lanka

Message

On the occasion of the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People, I reaffirm the
steadfast support of the Government and people of Sri Lanka for the inalienable rights of the
Palestinian people, including their right to self-determination and to an independent State of Palestine.

A year ago, the ceasefire in Gaza offered renewed hope following two years of immense loss of life
and suffering. However, despite these welcome developments, Palestinians continue to lose their lives,
while much of Gaza's population remains displaced, with families still deprived of adequate shelter
and access to essential services.

On this significant day, we urge all parties to fully uphold the ceasefire and call for the unhindered
delivery of humanitarian assistance to all those in need. Dialogue and diplomacy remain the only
viable pathways towards achieving a just and lasting peace.

Sri Lanka welcomes international efforts, including those undertaken through the Board of Peace, to
advance Gaza’s recovery and reconstruction. Such efforts must translate into tangible relief for the
people of Gaza while supporting a credible pathway towards Palestinian self-governance and
statehood.

Sri Lanka remains deeply concerned over the continued expansion of settlements and settler violence
in the occupied West Bank, including East Jerusalem. These developments further undermine the
viability of a two-State solution and jeopardise the prospects for a just and lasting peace.

Sri Lanka reiterates its firm support for a two-State solution, based on the establishment of an
independent and sovereign State of Palestine within the pre-1967 borders, in accordance with relevant
United Nations resolutions and international law.

On behalf of the Government and people of Sri Lanka, I reaffirm our unwavering solidarity with the

Palestinian people and our continued commitment to the realisation of their legitimate and inalienable
right to self-determination and to an independent State of Palestine.

[signature]
Anura Kumara Dissanayake
