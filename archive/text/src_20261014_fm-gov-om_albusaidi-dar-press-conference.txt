Minister and his Pakistani counterpart hold press conference
14 October 2026
Oman’s Foreign Minister Sayyid Badr Albusaidi and Mohammad Ishaq Dar, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, held a joint press conference in Islamabad following the eighth session of the Joint Ministerial Committee between the Sultanate of Oman and the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
Sayyid Badr highlighted the importance of dialogue, diplomacy and constructive engagement in addressing regional challenges, reducing tensions and resolving disagreements to prevent further escalation.
He commended Pakistan’s constructive role in supporting diplomatic efforts in the region, noting their importance in building confidence and advancing peaceful solutions to regional challenges.
Mohammad Ishaq Dar said the Ministers exchanged views on the war between the United States and Iran, the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, developments in the Bab Al Mandab Strait and the situation in Gaza. He noted the close alignment between the two countries’ positions and called for intensified efforts to address these issues and restore peace and stability to the region.
During the press conference, the Ministers affirmed the importance of continuing diplomatic efforts and prioritising dialogue to resolve disagreements and reduce tensions in support of regional peace and stability.
