Prime Minister Roosevelt Skerrit said his government wants to explore possibilities of allowing Palestinians to settle in Dominica.
He made the disclosure during his contribution to the 2025-2026 National Budget on Thursday.
“The other thing I think we need to look at, we have the Palestinians, I believe that we will be opened to engage the Government of Qatar, the Government of Saudi Arabia and the government of UAE [United Arab Emirates] with a view to quite possibly allowing the resettlement of a couple hundreds of Palestinians into Dominica if we can get funding from these governments,” he said.
According to Skerrit, his government is engaging the United States Government in that regard as well.
“If we can get the infrastructure put in,” he stated.
He continued, “You have very talented people in this country. Top class doctors, top class engineers who we believe can assist us in our effort towards growing the economy and fill in the gaps where we have some skills deficiencies.”
The Prime Minister added, “And so we will be engaging the public on these issues and to solicit their feedback from the public on these things.”
Additionally, he said the world now is progressing, “and we have to be more open.”
Meanwhile, Skerrit revealed that very soon the government will articulate a more progressive immigration policy.
“One of the things we will be doing very soon is to articulate a more progressive immigration policy for our country,” he revealed.
He pointed out that every law Dominica inherited from the British is about prevention and not facilitation.
“And I believe that we have enough land in Dominica for an increased population, and therefore we have to be–in terms of visa regimes–I believe that we should look at eliminating all visas to Dominica for all citizens across the world and to facilitate people coming in…,” Skerrit revealed.
The Parliament of Dominica has been adjourned Sine Die.
