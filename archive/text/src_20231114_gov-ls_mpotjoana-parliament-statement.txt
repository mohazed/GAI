MASERU
NOVEMBER, 2023
Mohlomphehi Motsamaisi oa Lipuisano,
Bahlomphehi Litho tse Khabane tsa Ntlo ea Sechaba
Ke ema kapele ho lona letsatsing la kajeno ho etsa polelo
holima ntoa e ntseng e kupa lipakeng tsa naha ea Israele
le Palestina. Le tla hopola hore ka la bosupa khoeling ea
Mphalane selemong se holimo (7 October, 2023), ho ile hoa
qhoma ntoa lipakeng tsa naha ea Isreale le naha ea
Palestina, eo litlaleho li supang ha e qhololitsoe ke
sehlopha sa Hamas. Hamas ke sehlopha se hlometseng se
loanelang tokoloho ea naha le batho ba Palestina
khatellong ea naha ea Israele.

3 | P a g e
Litlaleho li tsoela pele ho supa hore letsatsing lona leo la
bosupa Mphalane, sehlopha sa Hamas se ile sa
its’ohlometsa ka hare ho naha ea Israele ka mokhoa oa
tsome, ‘me litlaleho li supa ha Hamas e bolaile MaIsraele a
ka bang sekete le makholo a mane (1,400) ba kenyeletsang
litho tsa ts’ireletso, le ho koetela MaIsraele a ka bang
makholo a mabeli (200).
Sesosa sa ntoa pakeng tsa Israele le Palestina
Litho tse khabane li tla hoopla hore pele ho selemo sa
sekete le makholo a robong, mashome a mane le metso e
robeli (1948), naha ea Israele e ne e so thehoe. Naha ea
Israele e ile ea thehoa ka sona selemo seo sa 1948 ka mora

Ntoa ena e qhommeng ka khoeli ea Mphalane ke e mahlo-

kaholimo ho likete tse leshome le motso o mong (11,000),

la Machaba a Kopaneng (UN General Assembly) ho ba le
tulo e ikhethileng ho lima litaba tsa Israele le Palestina (10th
Resumed Emergency Special Session) ka la 26
October,2023. Tulong eo ea lona, Lekhotla le ile la fetisa
qeto tse ‘maloa, ho kenyelletsa tse latelang:
