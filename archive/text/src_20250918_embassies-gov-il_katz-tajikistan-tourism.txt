This was the first visit by an Israeli minister to Tajikistan, and included high-level meetings, participation in the 2025 Tajikistan Tourism Investment Forum, and the signing of a Joint Declaration of Intent on Tourism.
During the official visit, Minister Haim Katz met Jamshed Jumakhonzoda, Chairman of the Tourism Development Committee under the Government of the Republic of Tajikistan. The two sides plan to hold talks on expanding tourism flows, developing joint initiatives to promote tourism, and attracting private sector investment in sustainable tourism projects.
A key event of the visit was Minister Katz’s participation in the Tourism Investment Forum in Dushanbe, which brought together government leaders, investors, and tourism industry representatives from Central Asia and beyond.
“Tourism is a bridge between our countries,” Minister Katz said ahead of the visit. “This declaration will lay the foundation for a dynamic partnership that will promote cultural exchange, economic development, and mutual understanding between the people of Israel and Tajikistan.”
The move is expected to open up new opportunities for businesses, tour operators, and hospitality professionals in both countries, and will help expand the country’s international tourism partnerships and diversify its inbound markets.
The visit marks an important milestone in relations between Israel and Tajikistan, reflecting a growing mutual interest in economic diplomacy and people-to-people ties.
