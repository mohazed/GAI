Addis Ababa, May 5, 2025 (ENA)—Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed conferred today with Israeli Minister of Foreign Affairs, Gideon Sa’ar, during his visit to Ethiopia.
In a social media post, the Prime Minister stated that: “I had productive discussions today on Ethio-Israeli relations with Israeli Minister of Foreign Affairs, Gideon Sa’ar, during his visit to Ethiopia.”
According to the Premier, the relationships of Ethiopia and Israel span economic, political, diplomatic, and social spheres, among many others.
“Our two nations continue to share a long-standing relationship grounded in historical roots and ancient connections. Today, our partnership spans economic, political, diplomatic, and social spheres, among many others” Prime Minister Abiy underscored.
