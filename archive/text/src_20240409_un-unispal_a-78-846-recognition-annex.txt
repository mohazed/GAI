Question of Palestine
Admission of new Members to the United Nations
Seventy-ninth year
Identical letters dated 2 April 2024 from the Permanent
Representatives of Mauritania, Saudi Arabia and Uganda to the
United Nations addressed to the Secretary-General, the President
of the General Assembly and the President of the Security Council
We write to you in our capacities as the Chairs of the Arab Group, the
Organization of the Islamic Cooperation (OIC) Group and the Coordinating Bureau
of the Non-Aligned Movement (NAM) at the United Nations in New York. Pursuant
to the declarations and outcome documents adopted by our respective Summits, held
in Riyadh on 11 November 2023 and Kampala on 19 and 20 January 2024, we take
this opportunity to inform you of the support of the League of Arab States, OIC and
NAM for the admission of the State of Palestine as a Member State of the United
Nations.
As reaffirmed in relevant declarations and final outcome documents, our
respective Groups and Movement support the application for admission by the State
of Palestine, which was submitted in September 2011 and which remains pending a
formal recommendation from the Security Council to the General Assembly to that
effect.
In this connection, we wish to bring to your attention that, as of this date, 140
Member States have recognized the State of Palestine, and we hereby enclose with
the present letter the list of these States along with the dates of recognition, and
corresponding group affiliations (see annex).
