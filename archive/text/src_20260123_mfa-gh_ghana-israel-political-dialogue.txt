HONOURABLE JAMES GYAKYE QUAYSON LEADS GHANA’S DELEGATION TO 3RD GHANA-ISRAEL POLITICAL DIALOGUE
By mfa-masterJanuary 23, 2026News
The 3rd Session of Ghana-Israel Political Dialogue was held in Jerusalem on Wednesday, 21st January, 2026. Honourable James Gyakye Quayson (MP), Deputy Minister for Foreign Affairs led Ghana’s delegation. The dialogue was co-chaired by Her Excellency Sharren Haskel, Deputy Minister for Foreign Affairs of the State of Israel.
The dialogue provided a high-level platform for both countries to engage on matters of mutual interest, including diplomatic cooperation, trade and investment, security, innovation, agriculture, and capacity-building initiatives.
Discussions underscored the longstanding cordial relations between Ghana and Israel and the shared desire to deepen collaboration for mutual benefit.
The Ghanaian delegation highlighted Ghana’s priorities and opportunities for partnership, while both sides expressed satisfaction with the progress made since the previous political dialogue and agreed to sustain regular consultations.

In consonance with Article 73 of the 1992 Constitution of the Republic of Ghana, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs is the principal organ of state responsible for the administrative and proactive initiation, formulation, coordination and management of Ghana’s Foreign Policy. The Ministry comprises the Headquarters, fifty one (51) Diplomatic Missions, six (6) Consulate-Generals and fifty eight (58) Honorary Consulates.
