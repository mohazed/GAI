Prime Minister Abiy Holds Phone Call with Israeli Counterpart Netanyahu
Addis Ababa, October 12, 2026 (ENA) — Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed underscored that he held a “fruitful and constructive” telephone conversation with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, discussing bilateral relations and key regional developments.
In a social media post, PM Abiy said the two leaders deliberated on areas of mutual interest and reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening cooperation.
