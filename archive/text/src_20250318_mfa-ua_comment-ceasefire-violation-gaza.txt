Comment of the MFA of Ukraine on the ceasefire violation in Gaza Strip
Comment of the MFA of Ukraine on the ceasefire violation in Gaza Strip
18 March 2025 19:44
Foreign Affairs
We follow with great concern the fresh outbreak of violence in Gaza, which has already resulted in numerous civilian casualties.
We urge all parties to uphold their commitments and avoid further violence, which could have disastrous consequences for the region.
Instead, the ceasefire must be renewed, all hostages freed, and humanitarian aid to Gaza residents resumed.
