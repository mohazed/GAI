Assalamualaikum and Ramadan Mubarak
At the outset, I would like to express my sincere appreciation to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia for convening this important Open-Ended Executive Committee Meeting. We commend the leadership and unwavering commitment of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation in mobilizing collective efforts of the OIC Member States to address the ongoing grave situation in Palestine.
Having recently assumed my present responsibilities, I am honoured to join you and to convey, on behalf of our Leader His Excellency Tarique Rahman, the Hon’ble Prime Minister of Bangladesh, warm greetings and best wishes to the Ummah during the blessed month of Ramadan. Bangladesh remains committed to strengthening cooperation with all Member States, particularly our brothers and sisters across the Muslim Ummah.
Mr. Chair,
We have assembled at a critical moment to express our unwavering solidarity with the oppressed people of Palestine in the face of yet another unilateral and unlawful measure by the Israeli authorities. The recent legislation facilitating land acquisition by Israelis in the occupied West Bank constitutes a grave attempt to impose a fait accompli through expansion of colonial settlements. This action is clearly designed to alter the legal and demographic character of the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including Al-Quds Al-Sharif.
Bangladesh unequivocally condemns these measures which represent a blatant violation of international law, including relevant United Nations resolutions, and undermine the very foundations of a just and lasting peace as well as the establishment of an independent Palestinian State and the right of return of the Palestinians.
Honourable Ministers and Distinguished Delegates,
Bangladesh firmly believes that the only viable and enduring solution to this protracted crisis lies in the establishment of an independent and sovereign Palestinian State along the pre-1967 borders, with East Jerusalem as its capital.
In light of the recent Israeli legislation, Bangladesh calls upon Member States of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation to consider the following collective measures:
First, to categorically reject and refuse recognition of any legal or administrative changes introduced by Israel in the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including measures related to land registration and ownership.
Second, to strengthen coordinated diplomatic efforts at the United Nations and other fora to ensure accountability for violations of international law and to press for the immediate suspension and reversal of the new legislation.
Third, to review and, where appropriate, recalibrate bilateral engagements and economic dealings to ensure that there is no support, direct or indirect, that benefits illegal settlement activities or entities involved in such practices.
Fourth, to enhance material, legal and technical support to the Palestinian people, including assistance aimed at protecting land rights, documenting violations, and supporting affected communities in the West Bank and East Jerusalem.
Fifth, to intensify efforts to secure permanent ceasefire and uninterrupted humanitarian access to Gaza and to mobilize robust resources for relief, recovery and reconstruction.
Excellencies,
I reaffirm Bangladesh’s steadfast and unflinching support for the just cause of Palestine. It is imperative that meaningful negotiations resume without delay toward the implementation of the two-State solution. We urge influential global powers and key mediators to act with responsibility and resolve in advancing the creation of a sovereign and viable Palestinian State.
Excellencies,
Before concluding, our delegation would like to draw your kind attention to the upcoming election of the President of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly, for which Bangladesh and Palestine presented candidatures. We have learned that the Palestinian candidature has been withdrawn. This leaves Bangladesh as the sole contender from the OIC family, the Asia-Pacific region and the Global South. You may kindly recall that we sought OIC support which was endorsed in the last Senior Officials Meeting. I would like to take this opportunity to once again solicit the support of all our brotherly OIC countries in all geographical regions and request you to also advocate for our candidature with all UN Member States.
To our brotherly Palestine Delegation, let us reassure that- you may no longer be in the race, but you have Bangladesh standing in for you: We will continue to remain steadfast in our unwavering commitment to your noble cause.
I thank you all.
