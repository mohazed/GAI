16/11/2026
His Excellency Ambassador Coly Seck
Chair of the Committee on the Exercise of the Inalienable
Rights of the Palestinian People
Excellency,
Greetings,
As we commemorate the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian
People, I am pleased to express to your Excellency and the members of your
esteemed Committee our deep appreciation and pride in your efforts to defend the
legitimate and inalienable rights of the Palestinian people, to promote their cause in
international fora, and to keep their legitimate national rights at the forefront of
international attention. We reaffirm the State of Kuwait's full support for the role
your committee plays in mobilizing international support for the Palestinian cause
and raising awareness of the Palestinian people's right to self-determination and to
achieve their freedom and independence.
On this occasion, the State of Kuwait, its leadership, government, and people,
reiterates its firm, historical, and principled stance towards the Palestinian cause,
considering it the primary cause of Arabs and Muslims. It affirms its unwavering
solidarity with the brotherly Palestinian people in their legitimate struggle to attain
their inalienable rights, foremost among which, is the right to self-determination and
the establishment of their independent state within the June 4, 1967 borders, with
East Jerusalem as its capital, in accordance with relevant international resolutions.
1
While we commend the steadfastness of the Palestinian people and their just
struggle, we follow with grave concern the continued illegal practices of the
occupying power in the occupied Palestinian territory, including illegal settlement
expansion, land confiscation, settler attacks and ongoing violations against civilians,
as well as attempts to alter the historical and legal status of Jerusalem and its
religious sites. These violations constitute a flagrant breach of international law and
relevant Security Council resolutions and undermine the prospects for establishing
a just and lasting peace.
In this context, the State of Kuwait emphasizes the importance of the advisory
opinion issued by the International Court of Justice on 19 July 2024 concerning the
legal consequences arising from Israel’s policies and practices in the occupied
Palestinian territory, including East Jerusalem, which clearly affirmed the illegality
of the occupation and settlements and enshrined the Palestinian people’s right to self-
determination.
Accordingly, the State of Kuwait emphasizes the necessity of activating
international mechanisms to implement the recommendations contained in the
advisory opinion, thereby ensuring the end of the occupation, achieving justice, and
guaranteeing accountability for violations committed against Palestinian civilians.
The State of Kuwait also stresses the need to reach a permanent ceasefire in
the Gaza Strip and ensure its sustainability, protect civilians and civilian
infrastructure, and guarantee the safe, regular, sustainable, and unhindered delivery
of humanitarian and relief aid. Furthermore, it emphasizes the importance of creating
the necessary conditions for early recovery and reconstruction, thereby alleviating
the escalating humanitarian suffering of the brotherly Palestinian people and
preserving their dignity and right to remain on their land.
2
The State of Kuwait reiterates its categorical rejection of any attempts to
forcibly displace the Palestinian people, whether within their land or outside it,
considering this a flagrant violation of the rules of international humanitarian law.
Kuwait emphasizes the necessity of preserving the unity of the occupied Palestinian
territory, both geographically and politically, and rejects any attempts to separate the
Gaza Strip from the West Bank.
Based on its fraternal and humanitarian responsibilities, the State of Kuwait
affirms the pivotal role of the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine
Refugees in the Near East (UNRWA), as an essential and indispensable pillar in
providing educational, health, and relief services to millions of Palestinian refugees,
and a central element in maintaining the social fabric and human stability in the
occupied Palestinian territory and the region.
The State of Kuwait stresses the need to protect the Agency from attempts to
distort, weaken, or politically target it, and to ensure its continued fulfillment of its
mandate in accordance with the mandate granted to it by the United Nations General
Assembly. Kuwait also emphasizes the need to provide sustainable funding and the
necessary resources to enable UNRWA to continue its operations and to guarantee
the safety of its staff and facilities, commending the immense sacrifices made by
humanitarian workers, including UNRWA staff, while affirmed that there is no
alternative to UNRWA as long as the Palestinian refugee issue remains unresolved
in a just and comprehensive manner, in accordance with relevant UN resolutions.
3
In this context, the State of Kuwait emphasizes that the continued failure of
the international community to put an end to Israeli violations undermines the
credibility of the international legal system and necessitates serious and responsible
international action to ensure respect for the rules of international law and
resolutions of international legitimacy, to strengthen the principle of accountability
and non-impunity, and to enable the United Nations and its humanitarian agencies
to carry out their mandates and tasks without hindrance.
The State of Kuwait reaffirms its commitment to the two-state solution as the
only path toward achieving a just and lasting peace. In this regard, it commends the
outcomes of the High-Level International Conference on the Peaceful Settlement of
the Palestinian Question and the implementation of the two-state solution, held in
New York in 2025, co-chaired by the sisterly Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the
French Republic, which resulted in the adoption of the "New York Declaration." This
declaration, which received the support of the United Nations General Assembly,
reflects a broad international consensus on the necessity of advancing toward a just,
comprehensive, and peaceful settlement of the Palestinian question.
In this context, the State of Kuwait underscores the importance of building
upon the outcomes of the said Conference and the New York Declaration, and of
following up on the implementation of the commitments contained therein. This is
essential to transition from international consensus to the adoption of practical,
tangible, and time-bound steps toward realizing the two-state solution, ending the
occupation, and establishing an independent, sovereign, viable, and geographically
contiguous Palestinian state based on the borders of June 4, 1967, with East
Jerusalem as its capital.
4
The State of Kuwait further emphasizes the importance of sustaining
international efforts to follow up on the commitments set forth in the New York
Declaration, and of building upon the international meetings and initiatives that took
place in 2026 within this framework. Such efforts will enhance the effectiveness of
international action and ensure that political pledges are translated into practical,
concrete measures that contribute to ending the occupation and fulfilling the
legitimate aspirations of the Palestinian people for freedom and independence.
In conclusion, the State of Kuwait remains, as it has always been, a staunch
advocate for truth and justice, and a steadfast supporter of the Palestinian people,
until they regain all their legitimate political rights and secure their independent
state, with East Jerusalem as its capital.
Please accept, Your Excellency, the assurances of our highest consideration.
Meshal Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah
Amir of the State of Kuwait
