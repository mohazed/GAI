SUBJECT: Prohibition on the Carriage of Arms and Ammunition Destined for
Use in Conflict Zones
REFERENCE:
a) Antigua and Barbuda Merchant Shipping Act (MSA) 2006 and related
Regulations (Statutory Instruments, Directives and Circulars, as
applicable).
b) United Nations General Assembly Resolution A/RES/Es-10/24
c) United Nations Human Rights Council Resolution A/HRC/RES/55/28
TO: Ship-owners, operators, masters and officers of Antigua and Barbuda
flagged ships, and recognized organizations
1. INTRODUCTION
This Information Notice serves as a formal notification regarding the position of
the Government of Antigua & Barbuda on the carriage of arms and ammunition
on board Antigua & Barbuda-flagged vessels, destined for use in conflict zones.
2. SUBJECT
1. Prohibition
1. It is strictly prohibited for any vessel registered under the Antigua &
Barbuda flag to carry arms, ammunition, or other military equipment
intended for delivery to, or use in, a conflict zone.
2. This prohibition applies regardless of the nature of the voyage or contractual
obligations and is in line with the principles of the Government of Antigua
& Barbuda.
2. Consequences of Non-Compliance
1. Any Antigua & Barbuda flagged vessel found to be in breach of this
prohibition is liable to be de-registered under the authority granted by the
Merchant Shipping Act 2006. Additional legal action may also be taken,
depending on the circumstances of the violation.
2. Any Antigua & Barbuda flagged vessel found to be in breach of this
prohibition is liable to be detained at the request of the Administration in a
foreign port for removal of prohibited cargo, at the owners expense.

3. Reason for the Prohibition
1. This policy is in place to ensure that Antigua & Barbuda-flagged ships are
not used in a manner that could contribute to the escalation of armed
conflict, violate international sanctions or arms embargoes, or compromise
the neutrality and international standing of the Government of Antigua &
Barbuda.
2. Participation in the transport of military cargo to active conflict zones poses
significant risks to the safety of the crew, the vessel, and maritime security
at large. Moreover, it can result in severe reputational damage to the flag
State, ship owner and crew.
4. Action Required
1. All shipowners, managers, and master’s must ensure full compliance with
this information notice. Due diligence must be exercised in vetting all cargo
and charter agreements to ensure that no arms, ammunition, or military
equipment destined for use in conflict zones are carried on board.
5. For further clarification or to report suspected violations, please contact the
Antigua & Barbuda Department of Marine Services and Merchant Shipping
(ADOMS) directly.
