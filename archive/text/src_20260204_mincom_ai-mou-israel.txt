About Ministry
Based on Decree No.1464 of the President of the Republic of Azerbaijan dated 11 October 2021, the Ministry of Transport, Communications and High Technologies was renamed the Ministry of Digital Development and Transport. The aim was to widen the implementation of digitalization and innovation in the country, develop radio spectrum management, communication and information technologies, improve regulatory mechanisms and create a healthy competitive environment.

Activity areas
The Ministry of Digital Development and Transport is the central executive authority implementing state policy and regulation in the fields of transport (except for cases determined by the President of the Republic of Azerbaijan), including maritime transport and civil aviation, communications (telecommunications and post), e-government, high technologies (information technologies, microelectronics, nano, bio and other innovative science-intensive technologies)

MoU on AI Cooperation Signed During Rashad Nabiyev’s Israel Visit
4 February 2026, 09.21 am News
SHARE
As part of his official visit to Israel, Rashad Nabiyev, Minister of Digital Development and Transport, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on artificial intelligence (AI) cooperation with Israel’s National Artificial Intelligence Directorate of the Prime Minister’s Office. The ceremony featured a speech by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.
Following the signing, discussions were held on the future implementation mechanisms of the agreement.
The meeting also included an exchange of views on areas of mutual interest, including cybersecurity, digitalization, and the space sector.
