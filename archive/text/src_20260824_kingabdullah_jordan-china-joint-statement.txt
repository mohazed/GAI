24 August 2026
The Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan and the People’s Republic of China issued a joint statement on Monday, following talks between His Majesty King Abdullah II and President Xi Jinping, in Beijing.
Following is the full text of the joint statement:
“Joint Statement by the People’s Republic of China and the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan on Deepening the Strategic Partnership Between the Two Countries
At the invitation of His Excellency President Xi Jinping of the People’s Republic of China, His Majesty King Abdullah II ibn Al Hussein of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan paid a state visit to the People’s Republic of China from August 18 to 24, 2026.
During the visit, His Excellency President Xi Jinping and His Majesty King Abdullah II ibn Al Hussein held productive talks that reaffirmed their commitment to continuing efforts to translate the Strategic Partnership between the two friendly countries into broader practical cooperation across various fields, and to strengthen coordination and cooperation on international and regional issues of mutual interest. His Majesty also met with China Premier Li Qiang, and Chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress of China Zhao Leji, during which the two sides engaged in discussions on ways to further consolidate the historic Jordanian-Chinese friendship and deepen strategic cooperation.
The two sides issued the following statement:
The two sides affirmed that since the establishment of diplomatic relations between China and Jordan in 1977, and particularly following the elevation of bilateral relations to the level of a Strategic Partnership in 2015, the course of their joint efforts has seen steady growth, reflected in the strengthening of mutual political trust, the achievement of tangible results in areas of practical cooperation, as well as growing cultural exchanges and closer coordination in multilateral forums, all of which serve the interests of the two friendly countries and peoples, and are in line with their respective positions and priorities. In this context, the two sides agree to move forward in deepening the Strategic Partnership between the two countries, in conjunction with the ongoing preparations to mark the 50th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between them, which falls in 2027.
Both sides expressed their satisfaction with the positive atmosphere that prevailed during the visit, commended the advanced level that the Strategic Partnership between Jordan and China has reached in all fields, and expressed their commitment to continuing to strengthen cooperation that mutually benefits and serves the interests of the two friendly countries and their peoples. In this context, the two sides agreed to strengthen cooperation in various fields, including trade, investment, energy and minerals, agriculture, tourism, education and culture, health, media, human resources, and social welfare. They also agreed to explore ways to expand cooperation in emerging economic sectors such as green economy, digital economy, artificial intelligence, and advanced industries, as well as to strengthen industrial and development cooperation, in order to contribute to shared prosperity. The two sides also agreed that the digital economy is an important trend in global economic development and expressed their commitment to cooperating in this area. They emphasised the importance of increasing cooperation in the tourism sector and taking practical steps to achieve this, including launching direct flights between the two countries as soon as possible. The two sides also underscored the importance of exploring broader avenues for cooperation in the field of environmental conservation. In this context, the Jordanian side expressed its support of China’s bid to host, in the Chinese city of Xiamen, the permanent secretariat of the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS)’s Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction (BBNJ) Agreement (High Seas Treaty).
The two sides agreed to strengthen cooperation in the fields of law enforcement and judicial affairs, and to step up efforts to combat cross-border crime and organised crime.
The two sides expressed their commitment to strengthening coordination and cooperation in the field of counter-terrorism, and to working together to combat all terrorist organisations listed by the United Nations Security Council, reaffirming their steadfast and mutual support for each other’s efforts in the fight against terrorism.
The Jordanian side expressed its appreciation for the important initiatives put forward by His Excellency President Xi Jinping, including the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind initiative, the Belt and Road Initiative (BRI), and other global development, global security, global civilisation, and global governance initiatives. The Chinese side commended His Majesty King Abdullah II ibn Al Hussein’s important role in efforts to achieve security, stability, and just peace in the Middle East, and in promoting regional economic cooperation, thereby serving the cause of comprehensive economic development and strengthening global economic cooperation.
Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to maintaining coordination on issues of mutual interest and to continuing to strengthen mutual trust at the political level. The Jordanian side reaffirmed its commitment to the One-China principle, its support for China’s unity, and its recognition that the Government of the People’s Republic of China is the sole legitimate government representing China as a whole, and that Taiwan is an integral part of Chinese territory.
The Jordanian side expressed its appreciation for the vision set out by His Excellency President Xi Jinping in April 2026, embodied by the four principles aimed at safeguarding and strengthening peace and stability in the Middle East.
Both sides emphasised the need to consolidate the ceasefire in Gaza and to ensure the immediate, adequate, and unrestricted flow of humanitarian aid. The Chinese side commended the important role played by the Kingdom in delivering humanitarian aid to Gaza, emphasising the pivotal importance of the Jordanian land corridor in delivering humanitarian aid to Palestinians in Gaza. The Jordanian side commended China’s constructive role in support of efforts to alleviate the humanitarian crisis in Gaza. Both sides emphasised that any arrangements concerning Gaza must ensure that Palestinians are able to govern themselves and manage their own affairs, preserve the unity of the West Bank and the Gaza Strip, and pave the way for the Palestinian National Authority to resume its responsibilities in the Strip. Both sides emphasised the importance of supporting the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees (UNRWA) and enabling it to carry out its UN mandate.
Both sides expressed their deep concern at Israel’s dangerous escalation of the situation in the West Bank and East Jerusalem, including the increasing frequency of settler violence, land confiscation, and the construction and expansion of settlements, as well as attempts to alter the historical and legal status quo of Muslim and Christian holy sites in Jerusalem. Both sides dismissed as null and void all unlawful measures aimed at annexing the occupied West Bank or any part thereof, which constitute a breach of international law and relevant UN resolutions. Both sides affirmed that the two-state solution is the only way to achieve just and lasting peace, and emphasised the need to take practical steps, in accordance with a clear timetable, to establish an independent, sovereign Palestinian state on the 4 June 1967 lines, with East Jerusalem as its capital, so that it may live in security and peace alongside Israel, in accordance with international law and relevant UN resolutions. They emphasised the need to support Palestine’s accession to full membership of the United Nations. The Jordanian side expressed its appreciation of China’s steadfast support for the legitimate rights of the Palestinian people and the considerable efforts it has made to support the peace process.
The Chinese side expressed its support for the historic role of the Hashemite Custodianship, exercised by His Majesty King Abdullah II ibn Al Hussein, over Muslim and Christian holy sites in Jerusalem, and for its role in preserving the historical and legal status quo there.
Both sides underscored the importance of upholding the ceasefire between the United States of America and the Islamic Republic of Iran, and reaching a comprehensive solution that addresses all causes of tension through dialogue and negotiation, whilst ensuring respect for the sovereignty, security, and territorial integrity of the countries of the region, and emphasised that the Strait of Hormuz is a vital international corridor for trade in goods and energy, and that normal shipping traffic in the Strait must be restored.
Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to upholding the international system, in which the United Nations plays a central role, and to safeguarding the international order based on international law and the fundamental principles governing international relations, in accordance with the purposes and principles of the Charter of the United Nations. They also stressed the importance of respecting the sovereignty and territorial integrity of states, the principles of good neighbourliness and non-intervention in their internal affairs, and the resolution of disputes between states through dialogue and diplomacy.
Both sides emphasised the need to respect international humanitarian law and not to politicise human rights issues. The Chinese side expressed its support for Jordan in hosting the High-Level Conference on Humanity in War, under the Global Initiative to Galvanise Political Commitment to International Humanitarian Law, which will contribute to strengthening joint efforts aimed at ensuring adherence to and implementation of international humanitarian law.
Both sides expressed their satisfaction with the success of the visit and the positive outcomes it yielded, which have helped to strengthen cooperation and relations between the two countries.
His Majesty King Abdullah II ibn Al Hussein also expressed his sincere thanks and appreciation to His Excellency President Xi Jinping, and to the Government and people of China, for the warm welcome and generous hospitality he was accorded. At the conclusion of the visit, His Majesty extended an invitation to His Excellency President Xi Jinping to visit Jordan at a time to be agreed upon by both sides.
During the visit, the two leaders witnessed the signing of a number of Agreements and Memorandums of Cooperation which will help to strengthen cooperation between the two friendly countries.”
