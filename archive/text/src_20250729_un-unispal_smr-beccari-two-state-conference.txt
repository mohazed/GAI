Statement by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Political Affairs,
International Economic Cooperation and Digital Transition of the
Republic of San Marino
H.E. Luca Beccari
High-Level Conference on the Peaceful Resolution of the Palestinian
Question and the Implementation of the Two-State Solution
United Nations, New York | 28–30 July 2025
Mr. President,
Mr. Secretary-General,
Excellencies,
Distinguished Colleagues,
On behalf of the Government of the Republic of San Marino,
I would like to thank the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the
French Republic for co-chairing this High-Level Conference
at a time when the need for moral clarity and political courage
has never been greater.
San Marino condemns unequivocally the attacks of 7 October,
which caused tragic loss of innocent lives. At the same time,
we cannot remain silent in the face of the disproportionate
military response that has followed - marked by widespread
destruction, civilian suffering, and severe humanitarian
consequences - including the lack of access to food, clean

water and essential supplies, raising the alarming risk of
famine. This constitutes a grave violation of international law
and international humanitarian law. It is incumbent on both
parties to strictly respect these fundamental norms, protect
civilians, and preserve the possibility of a just and lasting
peace.
We come together not only as representatives of our
governments, but as human beings. The Secretary-General of
the United Nations recently spoke of a “collective moral
failure” in the face of the catastrophe unfolding in Gaza.
These words must resonate within us. More than 50,000 lives
lost, including thousands of children. A society torn apart. A
people deprived of dignity, security, and hope.
The tragic deaths of UN and UNRWA staff members,
humanitarian workers and journalists must not be tolerated.
This is not only a political crisis. It is a test of our shared
humanity.
San Marino stands unequivocally for an immediate and lasting
ceasefire, for the unconditional release of hostages, for the
protection of civilians and for full humanitarian access, in
accordance with international humanitarian law.
At the same time, we are deeply concerned by the continued
expansion of illegal settlements, violence against civilians, and

other unilateral actions in the West Bank, which threaten the
viability of a future Palestinian State and undermine the
prospects for peace. The unity and territorial continuity
between the West Bank and Gaza under the Palestinian
Authority are essential to any meaningful two-State solution.
However, we must look beyond the immediate emergency. It
is time to reignite the political horizon.
San Marino reaffirms its unwavering support for a two-State
solution: a sovereign, independent, and viable State of
Palestine, living in peace and mutual recognition alongside the
State of Israel, both within secure and internationally
recognized borders, and with Jerusalem as a shared capital.
We believe that peace is not only possible, but necessary—
and achievable - if the international community acts with unity
and resolve. In this spirit, we support a multilateral framework
led by the United Nations - building on the Madrid terms of
reference, the Arab Peace Initiative, and the Peace Day Effort
- underpinned by strong international guarantees and credible
security arrangements for all.
Co-Chairs,
On 15 May 2025, the Parliament of San Marino adopted
unanimously - a historic recommendation committing the

Government of our Republic to the recognition of the State
of Palestine by the end of this year.
This is not a symbolic gesture. It is the outcome of deep
reflection and a principled stance, grounded in the values that
define our Republic: the rejection of war, the right of peoples
to self-determination, and the primacy of international law.
The recognition process has already begun. The Ambassador
of Palestine has formally presented her Letters of Credence to
the Captains Regent, our Heads of State. The next step will be
the appointment of a San Marino Ambassador accredited to
Palestine, establishing full diplomatic relations.
We will also continue to support Palestine’s full membership
in the United Nations, as well as to affirm the need of
International Organizations’ presence to deliver full and safe
humanitarian assistance, especially for children, women, and
the most vulnerable.
San Marino urges all nations to take coordinated and
courageous steps. We cannot allow violence, despair, and
extremism to shape the future. We must act now to build a
peace grounded in dignity, accountability, and mutual
recognition.
Co-Chairs,

San Marino may be one of the smallest countries in the world,
but we believe that the values of justice, peace, and human
dignity know no borders. We stand ready to accompany this
irreversible process, ensuring full respect for international law,
compliance by all and sustained engagement through credible
multilateral mechanisms.
Let this conference be remembered not as a missed
opportunity, but as a turning point. Let us replace cynicism
with determination and silence with action – by implementing
the two-State solution and by defining a common framework
for the recognition of the State of Palestine, with clear, shared
milestones that can encourage all nations to take meaningful
steps forward.
Thank you.
