Reference: C.N.264.2026.TREATIES-XVIII.10 (Depositary Notification)
ROME STATUTE OF THE INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL COURT
ROME, 17 JULY 1998
MALI: WITHDRAWAL1
The Secretary-General of the United Nations, acting in his capacity as depositary,
communicates the following:
The above action was effected on 24 June 2026, with:
(Translation) (Original: French)
The Government of the Republic of Mali reiterates its commitment to the promotion and
protection of human rights and to the fight against impunity in all its forms. To contribute to attaining
these noble goals at the national and international levels, the Republic of Mali ratified the Rome Statute
of the International Criminal Court (ICC) on 16 August 2000.
Over time, however, the Government of the Republic of Mali has noted with regret that the
delivery of fair international criminal justice, which is essential to achieving the legitimate aspirations
that led to the establishment of the ICC, is regularly hindered by the exploitation of the Court for
political purposes.
…
***
The action shall take effect for Mali on 24 June 2027 in accordance with article 127 (1) which
reads as follows:
“A State Party may, by written notification addressed to the Secretary-General of the United
Nations, withdraw from this Statute. The withdrawal shall take effect one year after the date of receipt
of the notification, unless the notification specifies a later date.”
30 June 2026
