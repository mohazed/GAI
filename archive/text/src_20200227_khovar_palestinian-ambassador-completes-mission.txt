Palestinian Ambassador Completes His Diplomatic Mission in Tajikistan
27 февраля, 2020 12:10
DUSHANBE, 27.02.2020. (NIAT Khovar) – The Ambassador of Palestine Muhammad Tarshahani has completed his diplomatic mission in Tajikistan, reports the Tajik Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
On Wednesday, the Minister of Foreign Affairs of Tajikistan Sirojiddin Muhriddin received Tarshahani.
The meeting focused on cooperation between Tajikistan and Palestine in various areas.
Diplomatic relations between Tajikistan and Palestine were established on March 6, 1992.
