# Code of conduct

This repository hosts evidence and discussion about a war. People arrive with strong views. The rules below apply to issues, pull requests and Discussions.

- Discuss sources, events and methods. Do not attack people.
- No harassment, threats, slurs, or incitement against any person or group, including on grounds of nationality, religion or ethnicity.
- Do not post personal information about anyone, including the maintainer.
- Keep to the subject of the index.

The maintainer may edit, hide or lock comments and block accounts that break these rules. Decisions are final. Report a problem with GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or open an issue.
